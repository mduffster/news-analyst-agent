## Key Developments Since Prior Report (July 22)

### 1. Brent Crude Breaks $100 — Highest Since May

**[VERIFIED]** Brent rose to 100.22 USD/Bbl on July 23, 2026, up 6.54% from the previous day.

**[VERIFIED]** The price for a barrel of Brent crude oil, the international standard, jumped 7.2% to $100.88. It earlier touched the highest price in two months for the most actively traded Brent contract in the market.

**[VERIFIED]** The prospect that disruption could widen to a second sea sent global oil prices surging in one of the steepest rises of the war. Brent crude rose more than 6%, breaking through $100 a barrel for the first time since May.

*Assessment: This is a critical threshold. Oil has risen ~$28 from pre-war levels (~$72 in late February). Dual-chokepoint crisis now directly reflected in markets.*

### 2. Houthis Execute First Strikes on Saudi Tankers Since Blockade Declaration

**[VERIFIED]** The Iran-backed Houthis claimed their first attack on commercial ships in recent months, opening a new front in the US-Iran war that has already disrupted global energy supplies and rattled bond markets.

**[VERIFIED]** "We targeted two Saudi oil tankers, named Encelia and Layla, for their violation of the blockade decision issued by the armed forces," Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Saree said on Wednesday.

**[VERIFIED]** The state-run Saudi Press Agency, or SPA, confirmed the Encelia was targeted while transiting the Red Sea, resulting in a fire at the bow of the vessel. Citing an unnamed official from the Transport General Authority, the agency reported all crew were safe.

**[REPORTED]** The Houthi also claimed to struck a Bahri owned VLCC Layla and that a fire also broke out on the vessel, however this attack has not been independently confirmed.

**[VERIFIED]** The Iranian-backed militant group also noted it forced approximately 10 ships to retreat and return.

*Assessment: The Houthis have transitioned from warnings to kinetic action. The second major chokepoint (Bab al-Mandeb) is now under active attack. Saudi's Red Sea oil export route is threatened.*

### 3. Tanker Explodes in Hormuz Mine Field — IRGC Claims Strait "Completely Closed"

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said early Thursday that an oil tanker was hit by an explosion in the Strait of Hormuz, forcing two other vessels to turn back after attempting to transit a mine-laden southern route in the strategic waterway. The IRGC said the three tankers had attempted to use the southern route through the Strait of Hormuz, which it said had been encouraged by the US military.

**[OFFICIAL]** The IRGC said the Strait of Hormuz would remain "completely closed" for the duration of ongoing US military operations.

**[OFFICIAL]** "The powerful IRGC Navy emphasizes that the Strait of Hormuz is under our control and is completely closed as long as the evils of the United States in the region continue, and no oil tanker will enter or exit."

*Assessment: Iran is demonstrating its ability to enforce the Hormuz closure through mines. The IRGC statement confirms their intent to maintain total closure indefinitely.*

### 4. US Deploys B-1 Bombers — Major Escalation Signal

**[VERIFIED]** The U.S. military used a B-1 long-range bomber on Tuesday to strike IRGC targets in Iran, according to U.S. officials. This was the first time that the United States conducted a B-1 mission since fighting with Iran resumed.

**[VERIFIED]** Using B-1 bombers, which can carry two dozen 2,000-lb. bombs or dozens of cruise missiles, signaled a significant escalation and expansion of the U.S. military campaign.

**[VERIFIED]** According to Axios, the deployed B-1 began its mission from a base in the United Kingdom.

**[VERIFIED]** The B-1B Lancer heavy strategic bomber has returned to action over Iran. According to a report by Axios on Thursday morning, this marks the first use of the aircraft since the ceasefire took effect in early April.

*Assessment: B-1 deployment signals significant escalation in strike capacity. This aircraft class was used extensively in Operation Epic Fury. Its return suggests preparation for heavier strikes.*

### 5. US Strikes Iran for 12th Consecutive Night

**[VERIFIED]** The United States continued to hit Iran for a 12th consecutive night early on Thursday, and Iran retaliated, striking targets across the Gulf, particularly in Kuwait and Jordan.

**[VERIFIED]** CENTCOM launched a 12th consecutive night of strikes on Iran on Wednesday.

*Assessment: Strike campaign continues without pause. Pattern of nightly strikes now firmly established.*

### 6. Iran Strikes US Bases in Kuwait and Jordan

**[VERIFIED]** The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) and the Islamic Republic of Iran Army (Artesh) issued separate statements on Thursday, July 23, claiming to have launched drone strikes targeting several U.S. military bases in Jordan and Kuwait.

**[VERIFIED]** In a statement issued by its public relations department, the Army declared that its recent operations targeted Camp Al-Doha, Ali Al Salem Air Base, and Camp Arifjan in Kuwait.

**[VERIFIED]** Jordan and Kuwait both reported intercepting strikes as Iran's military and Revolutionary Guards claimed to be targeting US assets in both countries.

*Assessment: Iran maintains retaliatory posture against US regional bases. The "eye for an eye" pattern continues.*

### 7. Trump Threatens "Major Military Punishment" for Houthis/Iran

**[OFFICIAL]** "If they do this again, the U.S. will hold Iran responsible, in that the Houthis are a Surrogate and/or Proxy of Iran, and major military punishment will be inflicted upon Iran and, of course, the Houthis, themselves," Trump wrote on Truth Social.

**[OFFICIAL]** President Trump said Thursday he is "disappointed" in the Houthis, an Iran-backed faction in Yemen, for shooting at two Saudi oil tankers and vowed to punish the militants and their Tehran sponsors if they strike again. The president said he attacked the Houthis when they caused trouble for commercial shipping last year and, "since that time, and during our conflict with Iran, they have acted very responsibly."

*Assessment: Trump is drawing a direct line between Houthi actions and US punishment of Iran. This sets up potential major escalation if Houthis continue Red Sea attacks.*

### 8. Rubio: US Policy is "A Head for an Eye"

**[OFFICIAL]** US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Thursday President Donald Trump's approach to Iran was "a head for an eye," warning Tehran it would face severe consequences for attacks on Americans or U.S. interests.

**[OFFICIAL]** "The president's policy is a head for an eye," Rubio told reporters at an ASEAN summit in Manila. "They will pay a very heavy price for the things they're doing. They're already paying a very heavy price," he said.

**[OFFICIAL]** U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio told a diplomatic gathering in Southeast Asia the price Iran pays "will get higher every night" until Tehran is ready for a peace deal it would live by.

**[OFFICIAL]** Rubio said Iran's industrial and defense industrial bases had been "decimated" and that Tehran was continuing to lose missile launchers and radar systems.

*Assessment: The "head for an eye" formulation is significant — it explicitly indicates disproportionate retaliation as policy. This signals escalation dominance strategy.*

### 9. US Surging Forces, Medics, Special Operations to Region

**[VERIFIED]** The U.S. is surging forces, medics and weaponry to the Middle East to give President Trump more muscular military options as he considers expanding the conflict against Iran. In the past week, special-operations forces have deployed to the region from their U.S. bases. Squadrons of jet fighters have been staged across the Middle East, and bomber aircraft at bases in the U.S. and U.K. are on high alert to ramp up operations.

**[VERIFIED]** There are signs the U.S. is preparing to ramp up military operations in the Middle East, including an increase in the number of refueling aircraft deployed to Israel, American B-1 bombers leaving U.K. bases, and dozens more medics being sent to the sprawling U.S. base in Landstuhl, Germany.

*Assessment: The deployment of additional medics is a notable indicator — it suggests expectation of increased casualties from expanded operations.*

### 10. Iran Vows Continued Retaliation; "Eye for an Eye"

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran vowed on Thursday to keep striking the region so long as it remains under attack.

**[VERIFIED]** Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi responded that Iran's defense doctrine is an "eye for an eye" and threatened countries that actively support U.S. attacks against Iran.

**[VERIFIED]** The incident came hours after President Donald Trump warned that the US would strike an Iranian bridge or power plant every time Tehran attacked a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, signaling a further escalation following the collapse of the US-Iran ceasefire. Iran responded by warning it would retaliate against US-linked infrastructure and energy assets across the region if Washington followed through on its threats.

*Assessment: Escalation spiral continues. Both sides signaling they will match or exceed the other's attacks.*
