### 2) Israel publicly signals readiness to re-strike Iran (“third time” framing)
- Israel’s Defense Minister **Israel Katz said the IDF is on alert and prepared to resume the campaign and strike Iran “even for a third time”** (variants of this quote are being carried across multiple outlets). **[REPORTED]** ([arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2650291/middle-east?utm_source=openai))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
This adds a **second escalation vector** (Israel, not only the U.S.) to the near-term risk set, and makes it harder for Iran to view the conflict as a single-channel “U.S. maritime enforcement” problem. Even if no strike follows immediately, the statement can **tighten Israel’s commitment posture** and shorten decision timelines if another shipping/base incident occurs. **[REPORTED]** ([arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2650291/middle-east?utm_source=openai))

### 3) Gulf-state diplomacy and navigation advisories are adapting in real time
- **UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed traveled to Kuwait** immediately after the Iranian attack, per AP reporting—an indicator of **rapid Gulf coordination** under direct threat conditions. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/4bf4fdd1f4d782ff08f60d152909faee?utm_source=openai))  
- A regional maritime advisory body (as quoted by AP) told mariners that **the “southern route” in the Strait of Hormuz has been expanded and remains available**, despite “recent unprovoked attacks on merchant vessels.” **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/4bf4fdd1f4d782ff08f60d152909faee?utm_source=openai))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
Two simultaneous signals: (1) Gulf capitals are **politically coordinating** to manage fallout and deterrence, and (2) maritime authorities are trying to **operationally preserve traffic flow** rather than concede effective closure. If the route expansion works, it modestly reduces the probability of a total shipping stoppage—but it also implies **a more militarized, managed transit regime** that can persist for weeks/months. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/4bf4fdd1f4d782ff08f60d152909faee?utm_source=openai))
