- **AP** reports U.S. and Iranian negotiators reached a **tentative agreement** to **extend the ceasefire by 60 days** and **launch nuclear talks**, with **President Trump still needing to approve/sign off**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:** Yesterday’s story was “talks in flux.” Today’s delta is **structure**: reported movement toward a **time-bound stopgap** (60 days) rather than a comprehensive settlement. The fact it’s pending Trump’s approval also raises the risk of **last-minute demands** or **walk-back**.
