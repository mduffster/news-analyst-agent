### Key Developments Since Yesterday

#### 1. Doha Talks Conclude — Pause for Khamenei Funeral

**[VERIFIED]** Iran and the United States concluded a round of indirect technical talks on Wednesday with no sign they had made headway towards a lasting peace. Instead, the two countries focused on issues that they said had been resolved when an interim agreement was announced two weeks ago.

**[OFFICIAL]** After two days of diplomatic talks, Iranian officials have left Doha as Tehran prepares for the multi-day funeral of its former Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. Those talks, held indirectly between the US and Iran, made "positive progress" and further discussions will be scheduled "at the earliest possible time" after Khamenei's funeral, according to Qatar.

**[OFFICIAL]** Funeral ceremonies are planned for July 4 through July 9.

**[VERIFIED]** The talks concluded without a breakthrough, focusing on issues that had supposedly been resolved two weeks ago. Sources said negotiators spent two days in Doha discussing maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz and financial incentives for Iran, two pillars of the initial agreement they signed in June, rather than more difficult topics that framework was supposed to tee up.

**[OFFICIAL]** The Doha indirect talks concluded after two days without a breakthrough but with implementation steps, including an arrangement to use part of the $6 billion in Iranian assets held in Qatar to purchase necessary goods for delivery to Iran and a communications mechanism to flag violations.

#### 2. NEW: US Reports 10+ Million Barrels/Day Through Hormuz — Iran "Caught Off Guard"

**[OFFICIAL]** Commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has surged over the past few weeks, with American military support helping boost oil flows to more than 10 million barrels per day, a US official said. The increase since President Donald Trump signed an interim peace agreement with Iran represents a big jump in traffic since the war paralyzed flows.

**[REPORTED]** The increase since President Donald Trump signed an interim peace agreement with Iran represents a big jump in traffic since the war paralyzed flows. That has caught Tehran off guard, underscoring its now-limited ability to halt traffic through the corridor and helping spark recent attacks around the strait, said the official, who requested anonymity to describe internal thinking.

**[VERIFIED]** The United Arab Emirates restored its exports to over 3.9 million barrels daily, pushing total daily Hormuz flows past 10 million barrels. This surge, combined with ongoing emergency reserve releases and ad hoc Saudi sales to Asia, has created a market surplus.

#### 3. NEW: Iran Issues Fresh Warning on Hormuz

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran's military headquarters warns any US interference in the Strait of Hormuz will be met with a "decisive and swift response" as tensions continue to roil negotiations. Qatar reports "positive progress" as the US and Iran conclude indirect technical talks in Doha on issues related to the memorandum of understanding signed on June 17.

**[VERIFIED]** Iran issued a fresh warning today for vessels to follow Tehran-designated routes through the Strait of Hormuz.

**[VERIFIED]** Iran is resolute in securing international recognition of its authority over the strait, even if it must resort to force, according to two senior Iranian sources. Iran has repeatedly stated that it will begin imposing tolls on shipping in mid-August, once the toll-free period stipulated in the original agreement comes to an end.

#### 4. Oil Plunges — Lowest Since Late February

**[VERIFIED]** Crude Oil fell to 67.64 USD/Bbl on July 2, 2026, down 1.38% from the previous day. Over the past month, Crude Oil's price has fallen 29.56%, but it is still 1.21% higher than a year ago.

**[VERIFIED]** Crude oil fell below $68 per barrel on Thursday, reaching its lowest level since late February as oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz continued to increase and investors welcomed signs of progress in indirect US-Iran talks.

**[VERIFIED]** Today's trading range for Brent Oil futures is between 70.13 and 71.30.

#### 5. Frozen Assets: Mechanism Agreed — But No Transfer Yet

**[OFFICIAL]** The two sides settled mechanics for a first tranche of frozen assets. Gharibabadi said it was decided that necessary goods would be purchased with part of the $6 billion in Iranian oil revenues held in Qatar and delivered to Iran based on its needs; Qatar's foreign ministry stated the $6 billion had yet to be transferred, US officials maintained that no frozen funds would be released until Iran fulfills its commitments.

**[OFFICIAL]** So far, US officials say no frozen Iranian assets have been released. Qatar as well has not acknowledged any such transfer and Iran attacked a tanker filled with Qatari crude oil this weekend during the crossfire in the Persian Gulf.

#### 6. Netanyahu: IDF Staying in Lebanon "For the Foreseeable Future"

**[OFFICIAL]** Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu told troops in the southern Lebanon security zone on Tuesday that the military would remain in the area for the foreseeable future, crediting the soldiers' work for the recent agreement between Israel, Lebanon, and the US that demands Hezbollah's disarmament to enable an Israeli withdrawal.

**[OFFICIAL]** Defense Minister Katz said Israel has "no territorial ambitions in Lebanon," but that the IDF will not withdraw "a millimeter" until the terror group is disarmed.

**[VERIFIED]** A security deal between Israel and Lebanon risks entrenching a stalemate rather than resolving Israel's underlying conflict with Hezbollah by tying Israel's pullout from southern Lebanon to the Iran-backed terror group's disarmament, a condition regional analysts and politicians say is unattainable.

**[VERIFIED]** Some 400,000 Lebanese uprooted by war have returned to southern Lebanon, with more expected to follow in the coming week.

#### 7. Khamenei Funeral — Massive Multi-Day Procession

**[VERIFIED]** According to reports from Iranian state media, farewell ceremonies will be held in Tehran on July 3 and 4, followed by a procession in Qom on July 5, and a transfer of the body to Iraq on July 7, before returning to Iran for final rites and burial in Mashhad at the shrine of the eighth Shia imam, on July 8. The funeral, originally scheduled for March, was postponed due to the war between Iran, the US and Israel.

**[VERIFIED]** An official ceremony for foreign dignitaries to pay their respects would be held in Tehran on 3 July. Officials from more than 30 countries had requested to attend, while religious leaders and scholars representing more than 90 countries had also expressed their intention to participate.

**[UNCONFIRMED]** Since being named Khamenei's successor, Mojtaba Khamenei has not appeared in public, and no audio or video recording of him has been released. It remains unclear whether the new leader of the Islamic Republic will attend the funeral and burial of his predecessor and father.

### Emerging Signals

**Hormuz data discrepancy requires monitoring:** The US official claim of 10+ million barrels/day through Hormuz contradicts the "5 ships vs 93 normal" tracker data. The 10M figure reportedly includes US military support and UAE workarounds. The reality may be that oil is flowing at higher volumes than vessel counts suggest (larger tankers, different routes). This needs confirmation but could fundamentally change the leverage calculus.

**Iran's "decisive response" warning amid traffic recovery:** If traffic is indeed surging despite Iran's objections, Tehran faces a strategic dilemma: enforce its claimed control (risking escalation) or accept de facto loss of leverage. The fresh warning suggests Iran is preparing to act.

**Khamenei funeral creates 7-day window:** Negotiations paused July 3-9. Iran is unlikely to initiate major military action during the funeral. This creates a brief stability window but also delays any progress toward resolution.

**Mojtaba Khamenei's absence question:** The new Supreme Leader has not appeared publicly since the February 28 strike that killed his father (and injured him). His participation or non-participation in the funeral will signal something about Iran's internal stability.

**Lebanon framework effectively dead:** Netanyahu's "foreseeable future" statement and the consensus that Hezbollah disarmament is "unattainable" means the framework has no path to implementation. The question now is whether it provides diplomatic cover for an extended status quo or collapses into renewed conflict.
