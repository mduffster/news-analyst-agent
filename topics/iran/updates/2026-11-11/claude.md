### **HEGSETH IMPEACHMENT BLOCKED — DEMOCRATIC ABSENCES DECISIVE** [VERIFIED]

The House voted 211-204 to send the impeachment effort against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively killing the movement to oust him before the end of the year. Democrats in part gave House GOP leaders a window to sideline the impeachment effort due to absences — eleven House Democrats did not vote on the measure, more than the seven-vote margin.

The final vote on Tuesday was 211 in favour and 204 against, with Massie the lone Republican to vote against it. "The Democrats' attempt to impeach Secretary of War Pete Hegseth has miserably FAILED!" Trump said in a social media post following the vote. "Every single Republican, except Thomas Massie, a major LOSER, voted in favour of Pete."

The reprieve is probably short-lived: Democrats are all but guaranteed to force the issue again when they retake majorities in the House and Senate in January.

**[ANALYSIS]** The GOP attendance strategy worked — but ironically it was 11 Democratic absences that proved decisive. This underscores how razor-thin margins create unexpected outcomes. Hegseth survives the lame-duck period, but faces renewed impeachment push in January under Democratic control.

### **TREASURY SECRETARY BESSENT MOVED TO MILITARY BASE — IRANIAN THREATS** [VERIFIED]

United States Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has recently moved from his home in Washington, DC, to an undisclosed US military base in the wake of "Iranian threats" to his life.

A spokesperson said "the secretary will not be deterred by the Iranian regime's threats and remains committed to executing Operation Economic Outcast and maintaining the blockade, which together are collapsing Iran's economy." Security concerns surrounding Bessent increased when The Wall Street Journal published an aerial view of the Georgetown mansion that he bought for $12.5m shortly after his confirmation as Treasury secretary.

Other Cabinet officials including Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth and Secretary of State Marco Rubio are currently living on military bases while in Washington. Bessent becomes the latest in a growing number of senior administration officials who have moved to military bases for their safety.

The threats come as President Donald Trump has chosen to intensify the sweeping economic offensive against Iran spearheaded by Bessent, rather than resume major military operations. A senior administration official subsequently confirmed the move to Fox News, revealing that the threats against Bessent had progressed beyond an advanced stage and directly attributing Iran's targeting of the Treasury secretary to his leadership of the administration's economic campaign.

**[ANALYSIS]** Three Cabinet members now on military bases (Hegseth, Rubio, Bessent). This represents a significant escalation of Iranian asymmetric pressure against US leadership. Targeting Bessent specifically because of Operation Economic Outcast signals Iran views the sanctions campaign as a primary threat.

### **IRAN THREATENS TO STRIKE US BASES IN EUROPE** [VERIFIED]

Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei has warned that Iran may strike US military bases in Europe if they are used to launch attacks on the Islamic Republic.

"If American aircraft take off from an American military base, even one located in a European country or some other distant region, to bomb Iran, we will strike those bases as well," Rezaei told Lebanon-based Al Mayadeen TV in an interview broadcast on Wednesday.

He also said that Iran's response to renewed US attacks would "target America's more distant military installations — including the ones we have not yet attacked," without specifying. "We may even strike certain islands that they assume are beyond the reach of our missiles," he added during the interview.

Iran's armed forces have prepared an updated list of potential targets covering locations in the region and beyond that could be used by the United States or Israel, deputy chief of staff Keyoumars Heydari said.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is an escalation of Iranian deterrence messaging. Combined with the RAF Mildenhall evacuation yesterday and RAF Fairford earlier, Iran is clearly signaling capacity to threaten European basing. This contextualizes the DOJ charges below.

### **DOJ CHARGES 3 IRANIAN OPERATIVES IN RAF FAIRFORD TERROR PLOT** [VERIFIED]

The Justice Department announced charges against three Iranian-backed operatives for their roles in nearly 20 attacks and attempted attacks throughout Europe and the United States, and a September 2026 terrorist plot at a British Royal Air Force Base in Fairford, United Kingdom, where American armed forces were stationed.

The charges announced on Tuesday centre on RAF Fairford in southwest England, where US forces and bombers had been stationed. The US had used the base to deploy bombers in its war against Iran.

From a jail in New York, a commander of an Iranian terrorist proxy group helped direct a failed plot to attack a key UK air force base used by the US to execute the war with Iran, federal prosecutors allege.

"These defendants helped orchestrate a campaign of terror on behalf of terrorist organisations affiliated with Iran that targeted Jewish communities in Europe and the United States, and the British military base, RAF Fairford, housing American service members," said Manhattan US Attorney Jamie McDonald. "The threat presented by Iran and Iran-backed terrorist groups to America and our allies is not hypothetical – it is very real."

**[ANALYSIS]** This confirms the September terror plot against RAF Fairford was Iranian-directed. Combined with Rezaei's threat to strike European bases, Iran is demonstrating both kinetic and covert capabilities to target Western military infrastructure beyond the Middle East.

### **IRAN ATTACKS NAVIGATION SYSTEMS IN OMAN** [REPORTED]

Iran attacked navigation systems on Didamar Island in the Strait of Hormuz. The impact on shipping is likely to be limited.

This indicates Tehran's ability to attack infrastructure used to restore oil flows in the Persian Gulf. According to estimates by two representatives of the shipping industry and a source in the maritime security sector, the attack is likely to have a limited impact on commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. This is because alternative navigation systems are available elsewhere.

**[ANALYSIS]** Iran is demonstrating capacity to degrade regional infrastructure even if immediate impact is limited. Targeting Omani territory — a neutral party — signals willingness to expand pressure.

### **RUBIO AT G7: "NO NEED FOR MAJOR COMBAT OPERATIONS" — IRAN REJECTS DEMANDS** [VERIFIED]

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio yesterday said that the Trump administration's economic pressure campaign against Iran was effective enough that "there's really no need for major combat operations any longer." Rubio added that the United States might still respond with force if Iran "crosses certain red lines."

Rezaei said the Strait of Hormuz – the global maritime corridor that Iran closed – will not reopen until the US meets Tehran's seven conditions. Iran has not released the full list of conditions. But Rezaei and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi have said it includes lifting sanctions and the US naval blockade, and ending the fighting on all fronts, especially Israel's attacks on Lebanon.

Rubio warned however there had been "no progress" in talks with Iran whose demands he said were "simply not just unacceptable but, in some cases, outrageous and ridiculous and really not realistic".

US President Donald Trump said the conflict with Iran would end soon but insisted Washington wanted a complete victory, saying Tehran would need 25 years to rebuild what US forces had destroyed. Trump said American forces had already defeated Iran militarily and suggested Washington could decide when to bring the conflict to an end.

**[ANALYSIS]** Clear divergence: US says economic pressure is working and no need for combat; Iran says Hormuz stays closed until all demands met. Neither side is making concessions. "No progress" from Rubio suggests diplomatic breakthrough remains distant.

Yashar party leader Gadi Eisenkot asks Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara to intervene so he can receive regular security briefings from caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, alleging that his team's attempts to arrange them with Netanyahu's staff have failed.

The appeal follows Eisenkot's warning to Netanyahu not to deploy security forces without an immediate threat, amid reports that the caretaker premier is seeking military action against Iran despite objections from security chiefs.

Israeli prime ministerial hopeful Gadi Eisenkot called on Wednesday for caretaker premier Benjamin Netanyahu to "show restraint and prudence" regarding security decisions, in an apparent reference to potential renewed attacks on Iran.

In closing, Eisenkot warned that controversial security decisions during the transition could place senior military and security officials in a difficult constitutional position. Netanyahu's office rejected Eisenkot's warning, calling his letter "an irresponsible move" and accusing him of politicizing national security.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is a serious constitutional clash. Netanyahu potentially seeking military action against Iran while denying security briefings to the incoming PM creates significant transition risks. IDF Chief reportedly also warning against action.
