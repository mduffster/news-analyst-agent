## Key Developments (Past 24 Hours)

### **VANCE LETTER TO REZAEI: DIPLOMATIC PUSH BEFORE MIDTERMS** [REPORTED]

US Vice President JD Vance sent a letter to Iranian Security Council chief Mohsen Rezaei in a bid to revive peace negotiations, The Wall Street Journal reports, citing US officials and people familiar with the matter.

The goal was to see if "Tehran and Washington could find enough common ground to persuade Trump not to give a final order for a short, intense attack on Iran in the coming weeks".

Vance reportedly proposed a meeting soon between U.S. and Iranian officials, with himself leading the American delegation, as he did during talks in Switzerland in June. According to U.S. and Iranian officials, the letter calls for direct talks and insists Iran's nuclear program remain on the agenda from the outset. If Tehran agrees, negotiations could begin as early as next week, with Qatar and Turkey floated as possible venues.

Tehran has received Vice President J.D. Vance's letter proposing new peace talks between the U.S. and Iran, according to the Islamic republic's media. Tasnim, a media outlet affiliated with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, reported that Mr. Vance's letter addressed to Supreme National Security Council Secretary Mohsen Rezaei has landed and that leadership is crafting a response.

The letter had been "received well by Iran's leaders", according to the New York Times.

Trump, however, denied instructing Vance to initiate peace negotiations with Iran, saying the vice president's communication concerned medical supplies.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is the most significant diplomatic development in weeks. Vance leading the outreach — rather than State Department channels — suggests a genuine push to find an off-ramp before post-midterm strikes. The "received well" report from Iran and the rapid Tasnim confirmation indicate Tehran is taking this seriously. However, Trump's immediate denial creates ambiguity about whether this is authorized diplomacy or a Vance freelance attempt to prevent escalation. The dual track — strike preparations + Vance letter — keeps maximum pressure on Iran while testing if a face-saving deal is possible.

### **ISRAEL ELECTION: FINAL COUNT CONFIRMS NO CLEAR PATH FOR NETANYAHU** [VERIFIED]

Israel's Central Election Committee said Thursday that the vote count in the 2026 parliamentary election had been completed. According to 99 percent of the votes entered into the system, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 seats in the 120-member Knesset, while opposition parties have 67.

The final results show: Netanyahu's Likud with 27 seats, Ben-Gvir's party with 7 seats, Yaakov Asher (UTJ) with 7 seats. Deri (Shas) with 6 seats, Smotrich with 6 seats.

For the opposition: Eisenkot's Yashar party with 21 seats, Bennett's B'Yachad with 12 seats, Yair Golan with 8 seats, and Lieberman with 7 seats.

Arab parties: Joint List with 11 seats, Ra'am with 8 seats — totaling 19 seats.

Eisenkot accused Netanyahu of launching an organized campaign to delegitimize Tuesday's election results, vowing to prevent any delay in the new Knesset's swearing-in.

President Herzog urged parties to come with an "open mind" to avoid another election: "We must do everything we can, certainly after yesterday, to avoid another round of elections. So I expect everyone who comes here to come with an open mind."

Herzog said he was expecting to receive the official election results next week from Central Elections Committee Chairman Supreme Court Justice Noam Sohlberg, after which he would start consultations with each Knesset faction over the formation of a new government.

Whoever gets picked gets 28 days to make it work; if they can't, someone else gets a turn.

**[ANALYSIS]** The final count confirms what exit polls suggested: Netanyahu's bloc fell far short of a majority (53 vs. needed 61), while the opposition + Arab parties combined have 67 seats. However, both Netanyahu and Eisenkot have rejected partnering with Arab parties. This creates three scenarios: (1) Grand coalition between Netanyahu and Eisenkot blocs; (2) Eisenkot-led minority government with Arab party support; (3) Political deadlock and fresh elections. Netanyahu remains caretaker PM indefinitely during talks.

### **OIL PRICES FALL ON VANCE DIPLOMACY HOPES** [VERIFIED]

Oil prices declined Thursday as prospects of renewed peace negotiations eased fears of supply disruptions stemming from escalating tensions in the Middle East. Brent crude futures fell 1.73% to $113.32 per barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude futures slipped 2.06% to $100.66 per barrel.

The pullback followed reports that U.S. Vice President JD Vance had reached out to Iranian Security Council chief Mohsen Rezaei in an attempt to restart peace negotiations, WSJ reported.

Brent oil is expected to trade at 120.58 USD/BBL by the end of this quarter, according to Trading Economics global macro models and analysts expectations. Looking forward, we estimate it to trade at 138.56 in 12 months time.

**[ANALYSIS]** Markets are responding immediately to the Vance diplomatic overture. The 1.7% drop reverses the prior day's gains and demonstrates how sensitive oil prices are to any signal of potential de-escalation. However, forecasts remain extremely elevated ($120+ by end of quarter), indicating traders still expect conflict to continue.

### **SAUDI ARABIA INTERCEPTS HOUTHI MISSILES; FIRE NEAR RAS TANURA** [VERIFIED]

The Saudi-led coalition battling Yemen's Iran-allied Houthi rebels on October 29 said it had intercepted a ballistic missile fired by the group into the kingdom.

Saudi Arabia has this month come under sustained attack from the Houthis, including a strike on Riyadh's international airport that was the kingdom's deadliest in years. According to the liveblog, the coalition destroyed two Houthi missiles fired towards Riyadh.

Oil prices have risen following reports of a fire near Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil export terminal amid renewed missile and drone attacks in the region. Brent crude futures climbed 0.6% to $116.50 per barrel earlier. According to Bloomberg, the fire broke out on 27 October, although the extent of the damage remained unclear.

**[ANALYSIS]** Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia continue unabated despite regional mediation efforts. ### **HEBRON: LOCKDOWN DAY 7 — "COLLECTIVE PUNISHMENT" WARNINGS** [VERIFIED]

The Israeli army is continuing its military lockdown of Hebron for the seventh consecutive day, carrying out raids and tightening restrictions in the occupied West Bank's largest city.

The seventh day of siege continues with a concurrent absolute blockade on Al-Jalda and Ein Deir Bahha quarters, with over 780 families (approx. 5,000 residents) remaining forcibly displaced.

On Thursday, October 29, 2026, Israeli occupation forces accompanied by heavy military bulldozers raided the commercial center of Bab al-Zawiya in central Hebron, forcing local merchants to close their shops and launching tear gas into dense civilian quarters.

This mechanism enables Israeli forces and settlers to usurp upper floors and rooftops above active Palestinian businesses outside traditional settlement corridors. By seizing the vertical airspace without confiscating ground-level storefronts, the occupation establishes dual-layered control.

The Israeli NGO B'Tselem said the Israeli military's "operation in Hebron has nothing to do with security. It is an act of collective punishment."

UN Human Rights Chief Volker Turk said: "Collective punishment is a grave violation of international law. Threats to empty and erase entire neighbourhoods also raise serious concerns of ethnic cleansing."

**[ANALYSIS]** The Hebron operation has entered a new phase with infrastructure seizure tactics. Displacement figures have stabilized at ~5,000, but the "caravan" installations suggest Israel is establishing permanent control over commercial areas. The operation continues despite election results, confirming it operates independently of political outcomes.
