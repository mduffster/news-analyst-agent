## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Fri, July 24, 2026 (ET)**  
**New since Thu, July 23, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) Spillover risk widened: Kuwait reports inbound Iranian missiles/drones + border-crossing drone strikes
- **Kuwait’s defense authorities reported air defenses engaging incoming Iranian missiles and drones**, and separately said the **Abdali border crossing** (Kuwait–Iraq) was hit by “hostile drones,” with **material damage but no casualties**. **[REPORTED]** ([thepeninsulaqatar.com](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/23/07/2026/kuwait-says-abdali-border-crossing-targeted-by-hostile-drones?utm_source=openai))  
**What it means:** This is a meaningful geographic broadening of the active air-defense battle space beyond Jordan/Israel into the Gulf. Even without casualties, repeated attempted strikes on/near U.S.-linked facilities and critical crossings increases the odds of (a) a miscalculated hit producing casualties and (b) tighter U.S./GCC escalation ladders centered on air/missile defense + retaliatory strike packages.

### 2) Civilian-casualty reporting inside Iran ticks up (Ahvaz area)
- Iranian outlets (via secondary aggregators) reported a **deadlier strike near Ahvaz (Khuzestan)**, with **4 killed and 5 wounded** attributed to a U.S. missile strike. **[REPORTED]** ([globalsecurity.org](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/ops/iran-war-20260724.htm?utm_source=openai))  
**What it means:** If this higher-casualty pattern is sustained/confirmed by additional credible reporting, it raises pressure on Tehran to answer more visibly (or asymmetrically) and raises the risk of the conflict narrative shifting toward “civilian infrastructure/casualty” framing—historically a driver of retaliatory broadening rather than negotiated off-ramps.

### 3) Bab el‑Mandeb incident catalyzes wider political condemnation (EU + Arab/Gulf)
