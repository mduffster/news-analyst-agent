### 1. CRITICAL: Trump "Close to Decision" on "Massive Attack" Bigger Than Operation Epic Fury [OFFICIAL]

[OFFICIAL] President Trump told Axios on Thursday that he's seriously considering restarting major combat operations in Iran — including strikes that would be bigger than the ones carried out during Operation Epic Fury.

[OFFICIAL] Trump said: "I am considering a massive attack. Bigger than ever before. I am close to making a decision."

[OFFICIAL] Trump said Israel "would join in two minutes if I ask them to," but added that "we don't need anybody" to launch a new operation against Iran.

[VERIFIED] In a brief interview, Trump acknowledged that such a decision would have consequences and stressed he hasn't made a determination yet. Trump didn't give a deadline for his decision. Two other U.S. officials confirmed no call has been made and no new orders have been given to the military.

[OFFICIAL] Trump said the Iranians "want to negotiate" but are not ready to make a deal at the moment.

*Assessment: [OFFICIAL] This is the clearest signal yet of imminent major escalation. The "bigger than Operation Epic Fury" framing suggests nuclear facilities and regime targets are in the target set. However, two officials confirm no orders have been given. The key variable is Iran's continued defiance and whether Trump follows through.*

### 2. CRITICAL: Iran Formally Rejects US Ceasefire Proposal — Demands Hormuz Resolution [VERIFIED]

[VERIFIED] Iran on Thursday rejected a ceasefire proposal from President Donald Trump that was carried to Tehran by the Iraqi prime minister, according to Iranian and Iraqi officials.

[VERIFIED] The officials said Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's trip to Iran followed his recent visit with Trump at the White House. The details of the ceasefire proposal were not immediately clear.

[VERIFIED] Iranian officials said it was the only offer on the table, and that their government was not interested in a temporary deal that left the question of control over the Strait of Hormuz unresolved.

[OFFICIAL] Iranian FM Araghchi stated the problem is not finding mediators but rather "America's outlook, which is illogical, greedy, and controlling."

*Assessment: [VERIFIED] Iran's rejection of diplomacy and insistence on Hormuz control as a precondition eliminates the near-term ceasefire path. Tehran has made clear it will not accept any deal that doesn't address its fundamental demands. This hardens the trajectory toward escalation.*

### 5. CRITICAL: Israeli Cities Open Bomb Shelters; Iran Threatens Tel Aviv Strike [VERIFIED]

[VERIFIED] Several municipalities across Israel on Thursday said they had opened public shelters amid concerns over a possible Iranian attack following the escalation between the United States and Iran. Cities that opened shelters include Ramat Gan and Herzliya in the Tel Aviv metropolitan area, the southern Red Sea resort city of Eilat, and Karmiel in the north.

[VERIFIED] The mayor of Ramat Gan says he is reopening municipal shelters in the Tel Aviv suburb out of growing fears that Iran may renew attacks on Israel. "The assessment is that threat of missiles from Iran over the weekend has grown and is no longer negligible."

[REPORTED] Two Iranian officials cited by The New York Times said the next phase of the escalation would include a strike on Tel Aviv if President Trump follows through on threats to hit Tehran and its infrastructure directly, with Iran also expected to ask the Houthis in Yemen to impose a maritime blockade on the Bab al-Mandab strait.

[VERIFIED] Israel took part in the opening strikes on Iran when the current war began on February 28 and has largely sat out the latest round of fighting; Iran has not struck Israeli territory in recent weeks, even as it continues trading fire with US forces across the region.

*Assessment: [VERIFIED] Israel is preparing for potential direct Iranian retaliation if Trump's "massive attack" proceeds. Iran's explicit threat to strike Tel Aviv links Israeli security directly to US escalation decisions. Israel may be pulled into the conflict by Iranian retaliation.*

### 6. SIGNIFICANT: Gulf Spillover Intensifies — Iran Strikes Kuwait, Jordan, Bahrain [REPORTED]

[REPORTED] Iran's military said on Friday it carried out drone strikes against several American military facilities and bases in Bahrain, Jordan and Kuwait in retaliation for the latest U.S. attacks, while explosions were heard near an American base in northern Iraq.

[REPORTED] Air defenses intercepted six drones over Erbil on the morning of 24 July, briefly closing the airport, and the IRGC said an attack on a Kuwaiti telecommunications tower answered US strikes on Iranian telecom infrastructure. Iranian retaliation swept the Gulf periphery: the IRGC claimed strikes on a Patriot battery, an MQ-9 hangar, and an equipment warehouse at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base and on a THAAD radar, a Patriot system, and a C-RAM radar in Jordan.

[VERIFIED] On Thursday evening, sirens sounded in Kuwait, according to reports from state news agency KUNA. The Kuwaiti army reported that the country's air defense systems were intercepting "hostile Iranian drone threats." Local sources said that Iran had launched drone and missile attacks at the Al-Salem airbase in Kuwait.

*Assessment: [REPORTED] The conflict is expanding geographically. Iran is now targeting US facilities across multiple Gulf states. While most attacks are being intercepted, the cumulative risk of a significant penetration causing US casualties increases with each wave.*

### 7. SIGNIFICANT: IRGC Commanders and Missile Equipment Flew to Yemen on July 13 [REPORTED]

[REPORTED] Iran flew Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) commanders, military advisers and missile- and drone-related equipment into Yemen this month, according to four sources, in a move that suggests Tehran is seeking to strengthen the ability of its Houthi allies to threaten Red Sea shipping. Four sources said Iran transferred the IRGC personnel and military-related equipment on a flight from Tehran to Yemen on July 13.

[REPORTED] The two Iranian sources told Reuters that between 10 and 21 IRGC personnel, including senior commanders, were on the Mahan Air flight.

[VERIFIED] The reported deployment came days before the Houthis announced a naval blockade of Saudi Arabia and later claimed attacks on oil tankers.

[REPORTED] Yemen's information minister confirmed the transfers of Guards personnel and equipment, citing intelligence. "Their mission is to strengthen the militias' military capabilities and prepare them to threaten international maritime security in the Red Sea and the Bab al-Mandab Strait."

*Assessment: [REPORTED] If accurate, this report confirms direct Iranian coordination of the Houthi Red Sea campaign. The timing (July 13, one week before the Saudi blockade declaration) suggests premeditated escalation. This corroborates the dual-chokepoint strategy.*
