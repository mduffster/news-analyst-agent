The United States has made the abrupt decision to withdraw its bombers from the Royal Air Force (RAF) base Fairford in the western part of the United Kingdom, citing concerns about a potential attack.

The United States withdrew all twelve B-1B Lancer strategic bombers from RAF Fairford in the United Kingdom on October 4, 2026, returning the aircraft to their home stations after months of supporting U.S. operations against Iran.

When asked if there was a specific threat that led to the bombers being moved, Trump told reporters: "Yeah, I think we can say that. They didn't fly out by accident. He added that Washington "actually knows the people that made the threat." When asked if they are linked to Iran, Trump responded: "They would be linked to Iran."

### **SECRET CAMP DAVID MEETING ON IRAN STRATEGY** [VERIFIED]

**[ANALYSIS]** The timing and secrecy of this meeting—echoing the June 2025 pattern before Israel's strikes—suggests major decisions may be imminent. The dual focus on Iran and Yemen indicates the administration is considering expanding operations rather than constraining them.

### **FLYDUBAI ATTACK: UAE LABELS IT "TERRORIST ACT"** [OFFICIAL]

The prosecutor general of the United Arab Emirates has said a Flydubai co-pilot "attempted to carry out a terrorist act" during a flight earlier this week, using a crash axe against the pilot inside the cockpit. State news agency WAM quoted Hamad Saif al-Shamsi as saying on Saturday that the attacker of Wednesday's flight FZ1073 tried to take control of the aircraft's flight controls.

The co-pilot is an Omani national who had been removed from a previous job at Oman's national airline last year following concerns about his extremist views. A LinkedIn profile bearing the name Hamam al-Hammami said he worked at Oman Air for seven years.

International investigators are still working to determine a motive but an Israeli official said information from the inquiry suggested the suspect acted alone. Another Israeli source said that, at the moment, Israel does not know of any link to a group or government.

A counterterrorism team is investigating Australian links to Mr Al-Hammami. The investigation is being conducted by the Victorian Joint Counter Terrorism Team, comprising the AFP, Victoria Police and ASIO.

**[ANALYSIS]** [VERIFIED] The emerging profile is of a lone actor with extremist views who was previously flagged at Oman Air. Israeli sources saying they currently find no state or group link is notable given Trump's earlier Iran claims. This appears increasingly to be inspired rather than directed terrorism.
