## Critical Developments (July 22)

### 1. CRITICAL: Trump Announces Infrastructure Retaliation Policy [OFFICIAL]

President Donald Trump is threatening to attack Iran's critical infrastructure, writing on social media that the U.S. will bomb "ONE BRIDGE OR POWER PLANT" any time Iran "shoots at a ship in the Strait of Hormuz." Those targets include "those located next to, or in, the Capital City of Tehran," Trump specified.

Trump has previously threatened to attack Iran's electrical plants, water facilities and other infrastructure. Experts have warned that such attacks could constitute war crimes under international law.

A spokesperson for Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps (IRGC) said Tehran would respond to any future U.S. attacks on Iran's critical infrastructure by hitting "all the infrastructure still standing in the region."

*Assessment: [OFFICIAL] This represents a major escalatory commitment. Trump has created a public "commitment trap" — if Iran attacks shipping, he is now politically bound to strike civilian infrastructure. This dramatically raises escalation risk and closes diplomatic space. Iran's counter-threat to target regional infrastructure creates reciprocal escalation dynamics.*

### 2. CRITICAL: Trump Announces Imminent Strikes on Pickaxe Mountain Nuclear Site [OFFICIAL]

President Donald Trump said Tuesday the U.S. would target Pickaxe Mountain, an Iranian nuclear site that is under construction and buried deep below the ground, as the war escalates amid 10 straight days of strikes. "We'll be hitting that area pretty soon, and very heavily," Trump told reporters in the Oval Office.

President Trump says the US will strike Pickaxe Mountain "pretty soon." It's a site that's deep underground where, according to Israeli Intelligence, Iran moved its advanced centrifuges and part of its stockpile of highly enriched uranium.

The Wall Street Journal reported that Israeli intelligence assessed Iran transferred centrifuges, which could be used to enrich uranium, the essential process in building a nuclear weapon, to Pickaxe Mountain last fall. The facility has been under construction for years and is only miles away from Natanz, one of three nuclear site targeted by U.S. and Israeli airstrikes in a June 2025 operation.

Axios reports Trump's remarks "signaled that he is not interested in a ceasefire at the moment and is moving towards escalating the strikes on Iran."

*Assessment: [OFFICIAL] The Pickaxe Mountain threat introduces a nuclear targeting dimension that has been dormant since June 2025. This represents an expansion of war aims beyond Hormuz reopening. Technical challenges exist — the site is deeply buried — but Trump's specificity suggests serious intent.*

Air defenses in Jordan downed eight of 10 projectiles fired by Iran on Wednesday, according to statements from the Jordanian Armed Forces. Iran fired six missiles and four drones into Jordanian territory according to the statements. Eight of the projectiles were intercepted. Two Iranian missiles fell in remote areas.

Shortly afterward, Jordan's Armed Forces stated that four Iranian drones were also intercepted in Jordanian airspace. No injuries have been reported.

*Assessment: [OFFICIAL] The salvo size increased from yesterday's three missiles to today's six missiles plus four drones.
