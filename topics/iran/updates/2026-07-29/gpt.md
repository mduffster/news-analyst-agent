## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Wed, July 29, 2026 (ET)**  
**New since Tue, July 28, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) The “strike pause” effectively broke: Iran fired ballistic missiles at a key U.S. hub in Jordan; intercepts reported
- Iran’s IRGC **claimed** it launched ballistic missiles at **Jordan’s Muwaffaq Salti Air Base**, described as a key U.S. regional hub; **Jordan said five Iranian missiles were intercepted/destroyed**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/e31d249ba6443decdd3e63cd00f0fb84?utm_source=openai))  
- U.S. reporting (via AP) says **all Iranian missiles aimed at U.S. forces were intercepted**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8d2ae29300a8dc5495a4ce56c5312bf1?utm_source=openai))  
- Axios frames this as **Iran’s first ballistic missile attack on a U.S. base since Trump paused strikes** (pause reportedly began Friday). **[REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/28/iran-missile-attack-us-base-jordan?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** This is the clearest operational indicator since yesterday that the “give diplomacy space” pause is **no longer holding cleanly**. Even if the U.S. doesn’t immediately return to a high-tempo air campaign against Iran proper, the risk of **reactive escalation** rises because (a) ballistic missile use is inherently escalatory, and (b) it pressures Washington to “restore deterrence,” at least via proxy-target strikes.

---

### 2) The conflict widened on the proxy axis: U.S. and Saudi forces struck Iran-aligned militias in Iraq (reportedly killing ~20)
- AP reports **U.S.–Saudi strikes in Iraq** against Tehran-backed militias, with the Popular Mobilization Forces saying **at least 20 fighters killed, 32 wounded**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/e31d249ba6443decdd3e63cd00f0fb84?utm_source=openai))  
- AP also reports the **Iranian missile salvo preceded** the U.S.–Saudi strikes (per a U.S. official), with the official stressing the actions were “not connected.” **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8d2ae29300a8dc5495a4ce56c5312bf1?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** Regardless of sequencing claims, the net effect is to **shift the center of gravity** from the U.S.–Iran direct exchange back toward the “regional entanglement” pattern: proxy strikes/retaliation, Iraq political instability, and added pathways for miscalculation—especially if Iraqi militia attacks on Gulf energy infrastructure continue.

---

### 3) Trump–Netanyahu optics were “productive,” but events immediately undercut the de-escalation narrative
- Axios reports the Oval Office meeting was publicly framed as **“productive”** and lacked obvious public tension. **[REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/28/trump-netanyahu-oval-office-iran-war?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** The meeting may still have produced alignment on *intent* to scale down, but the near-term reality is that Iran (or Iran-linked actors) demonstrated willingness to **test the pause** with ballistic missiles. That reduces confidence that diplomacy alone can stabilize the next 1–2 weeks without parallel coercive steps (e.g., intensified air defense posture, proxy-target strikes, stricter maritime enforcement).
