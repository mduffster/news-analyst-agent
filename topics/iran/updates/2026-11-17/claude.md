## Key Developments (Past 24 Hours)

### **US STRIKES IRGC MISSILE LAUNCH SITE ON QESHM ISLAND** [VERIFIED]

American warplanes hit an Iranian missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the Persian Gulf on Tuesday, a US military official said, as the two sides intensified tit-for-tat attacks in the ongoing war.

"Earlier today, US fighter aircraft used precision munitions to strike an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) missile launch site on Qeshm Island in self-defense," a US military official told *The Jerusalem Post*. "The strike was in response to IRGC aggression targeting US personnel and assets."

Iranian officials said the US military conducted airstrikes on Qeshm Island in southern Iran on Tuesday evening, with locals reporting at least five explosions. Iranian news agency IRNA reported that "the sound of five to six explosions was heard in the city of Qeshm."

Earlier on Tuesday, Iran's semi-official Mehr News Agency reported that US forces had struck several locations on Qeshm Island, adding the assault caused no casualties or damage to critical and public-service infrastructure.

The latest operation is separate from earlier US strikes on Qeshm Island reported on November 2, which American officials had also described as defensive actions.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is a significant development — US offensive action against Iranian territory, even if characterized as "self-defense." The pattern of tit-for-tat strikes is intensifying, with both sides maintaining escalation while avoiding attacks that would cause mass casualties.

### **IRAN LAUNCHES DRONES AT US RADAR IN BAHRAIN — THIRD ATTACK IN 5 DAYS** [VERIFIED]

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said it launched drones at a U.S. radar system at Bahrain's Sheikh Isa Air Base on Tuesday, the third such attack on the Persian Gulf kingdom in five days after a roughly two-month lull.

U.S. Central Command, or CENTCOM, said the multiple one-way attack drones were intercepted without damage to U.S. equipment or injury to American personnel. Bahrain's armed forces said they intercepted and destroyed a number of Iranian aerial attacks before they could achieve their objectives.

The IRGC said its drones carried out a "precision operation" in response to repeated violations of Iranian airspace by U.S. military drones.

Iran is signaling that even U.S. airspace violations, not just direct attacks on its territory, will trigger retaliation against American forces, according to Hamidreza Azizi, a senior Iran analyst at the International Crisis Group.

**[ANALYSIS]** The resumption of Iranian attacks on Bahrain after a "two-month lull" represents a tactical shift. Iran appears to be expanding its definition of provocation — now retaliating for US drone surveillance flights, not just direct strikes.

### **TANKER TARA GAS CREW ABANDONS SHIP — ONE SEAFARER MISSING** [VERIFIED]

The crew of the Panama-flagged LPG carrier Tara Gas have abandoned ship after the vessel was attacked twice in the Strait of Hormuz. The Tara Gas was first struck while transiting the Strait of Hormuz on 16 November according to maritime security firm Vanguard Tech.

Indian Minister of Ports, Shipping & Waterways, Sarbananda Sonowal, said that in a "distressing development" the Tara Gas was subject to a subsequent attack and the crew were forced to abandon ship. "While 24 crew members have been safely accounted for, one Indian seafarer remains missing."

Oman evacuated 24 crew members from Tara Gas about 2 nautical miles off the Musandam Peninsula after it caught fire, Oman's Maritime Security Center said on Tuesday. India's external affairs ministry confirmed that the Tara Gas had been attacked on November 16, adding that 2 of the 24 Indian crew members were admitted to hospital in the port city of Khasab.

At least 22 Indian nationals have been killed since the war began.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is the most significant human casualty incident in the Hormuz shipping campaign in recent weeks. The pattern of double-striking a disabled vessel is particularly concerning — Iran is showing willingness to attack ships already in distress.

### **IRGC CLAIMS ANOTHER US DRONE SHOOTDOWN** [OFFICIAL — Iranian claim, unconfirmed by US]

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claimed on Tuesday that it shot down a US MQ-1 Predator reconnaissance and combat drone in the southern waters of Iran, near Qeshm Island. According to the Guard, this would be the eighteenth drone of this type that its forces have destroyed.

Iran's army's public relations office said the drone was "hit and destroyed" by domestically developed air defense systems operating under Iran's integrated air defense network. Separately, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said earlier that it had brought down a US MQM-170 drone intact south of Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz.

**[ANALYSIS]** Iran has claimed multiple drone shootdowns in recent days. If accurate, this represents Iran successfully contesting US ISR dominance over the Strait — but Iranian claims often exaggerate. The US typically confirms significant losses.

Brent crude futures traded at $91.66 at 04:30 GMT on Tuesday, down 78 cents or 0.8 percent, after a 3.55 percent fall on Monday to $92.44. The surplus story is in charge, and Brent is back at the bottom of its November range. Reference levels: $96 above, $88 below.

Oil prices declined on Tuesday, as expectations of a global supply surplus and recovering exports from the Middle East outweighed concerns over continued attacks on oil tankers and energy infrastructure in the Gulf region.
