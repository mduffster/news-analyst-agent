## What’s new / what changed

### 1) U.S. force posture: a *third* U.S. aircraft carrier has arrived in the Middle East
- **What changed:** AP reports **CENTCOM announced** the **USS Theodore Roosevelt** has arrived, joining **USS George H.W. Bush** and **USS George Washington**—making **three carrier strike groups** in/around the theater, plus a larger escort flotilla. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/us-iran-war-aircraft-carrier-middle-east-9858fc82c61c7a1d159e7bba72afe99a))  
- **What it means:** This is a meaningful escalation *in capacity and signaling* even if kinetic tempo doesn’t immediately spike. It improves U.S. options for: (a) surge strike packages, (b) persistent ISR/air defense coverage, and (c) convoy/escort support under the blockade. Net effect: **reduces near-term Iranian confidence that the U.S. is tapering**, and **raises the likelihood** that any new maritime/base attack is met with **rapid, theater-wide punitive action** rather than narrow “force protection only.”

### 2) Strait of Hormuz: UKMTO logs a new “ATTACK” incident (unknown projectile hits a tanker; engine-room fire)
- **What changed:** **UKMTO Warning 207-26 (16 Nov 2026, 0558 UTC)** reports a tanker was **struck by an unknown projectile** while **transiting outbound** in the **Strait of Hormuz**, causing an **engine-room fire**; **no casualties** reported; damage/environmental impact **unknown**. **[OFFICIAL]** ([ukmto.org](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/20261116-ukmto_warning_207-26.pdf?rev=b7a05cc809114a4c9bb82eb10734d893))  
- **What it means:** This is stronger than prior days’ Iran-claimed incidents because it’s a **neutral, operational maritime security log**. It reinforces that the Hormuz picture is not just rhetoric/mines narratives—there are **active attack patterns** continuing. This pushes the conflict further into a **maritime attrition / disruption loop**, increasing: insurance pressure, routing constraints, and the chance of **misattribution-driven escalation** (U.S./partners striking “likely launch areas” without definitive attribution).

### 3) Maritime danger messaging intensifies; AP says Iranian state media warns mines and claims multiple tanker damages
- **What changed:** AP reports Iranian state TV (citing IRGC Navy/army) warned shipping companies about mines and claimed **mines damaged multiple tankers**; AP also references reduced traffic and ongoing U.S. blockade activity. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/us-iran-war-aircraft-carrier-middle-east-9858fc82c61c7a1d159e7bba72afe99a))  
- **What it means:** Iran appears to be trying to **shape shipowner behavior** (deterrence-by-risk) while preserving ambiguity around attribution. Coupled with the UKMTO logged attack, it suggests a **sustained campaign of maritime pressure** rather than isolated incidents.

### 4) Third-party analytical consensus hardens on the Bahrain drone episode (still unverified “radar destroyed” claim, but the attempted strike signal persists)
- **What changed:** Two separate analytic outlets summarize that **Iran launched drones toward Bahrain** and the U.S. says they were **intercepted** with no damage; Iran’s “radar destroyed” claim remains **unverified**. **[REPORTED]** ([criticalthreats.org](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/iran-update-november-13-2026?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** The Bahrain node is staying “hot” in commentary, but **today’s real delta** is elsewhere: **carrier arrival** and a **UKMTO-confirmed Hormuz attack**.

### 5) Diplomatic channel: Pakistan says it will proceed with U.S.–Iran diplomacy efforts
- **What changed:** AP reports Pakistan’s foreign minister said Pakistan would proceed with **diplomatic efforts** to ease U.S.–Iran tensions with regional support. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/fff3bfdcebb7ef084f0f29dd04d239ee?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** This is *not* a breakthrough indicator by itself, but it’s a live sign that **regional intermediaries are still trying to build an off-ramp**, even as military posture and maritime incidents point the other direction.

## Bottom line today
The most important changes since Nov 13 are (1) **U.S. posture tightening** with a **third carrier** in theater and (2) a **new UKMTO “ATTACK” warning** in Hormuz involving an **unknown projectile** and **engine-room fire**.
