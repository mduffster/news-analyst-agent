### **SECURITY CABINET ALIGNMENT — NETANYAHU-ZAMIR "SEE EYE TO EYE"** [REPORTED]

Two Israeli sources told i24NEWS that "it appears the Prime Minister and the Chief of Staff see eye to eye" on the issue of Iran, despite earlier reports which suggested a disagreement between the two.

"Chief of Staff Zamir and Prime Minister Netanyahu presented the same conclusions regarding Iran and did not contradict one another during the discussion. There were no discrepancies in the data or the conclusions," one source stated.

On Sunday evening, Israel's Security Cabinet convened for an extended discussion on Iran. Two Israeli sources told the Post that "it appears the prime minister and the IDF chief of staff are on the same page" regarding Iran, amid reports of disagreements between them.

During the Security Cabinet meeting, held in the Operations Directorate's underground command center at the Kirya military headquarters in Tel Aviv, senior IDF officials said that the Iranian regime continues to invest significant resources in rebuilding its missile and drone capabilities, despite the country's deteriorating economic situation.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is a significant development that reframes the civil-military dynamic. The reported alignment could mean: (1) Zamir's concerns were overstated in prior reporting; (2) Netanyahu moderated his demands in the meeting; or (3) they agreed on the intelligence assessment but not necessarily on the response. The absence of an immediate strike authorization suggests the "alignment" may be on threat assessment rather than action. **This reduces the probability of immediate Israeli unilateral action.**

### **USS THEODORE ROOSEVELT ARRIVES — THREE CARRIERS IN REGION** [VERIFIED]

A third American aircraft carrier has arrived in the Middle East, U.S. Central Command said in a social media post Monday, making a dramatic show of military might as President Donald Trump keeps his options open for next steps in the Iran war.

It joins the USS George H.W. Bush and the USS George Washington aircraft carriers, as well as smaller Navy ships, which are in the region to maintain a naval blockade of Iranian shipping.

Three US aircraft carriers are now operating in the Middle East under CENTCOM's command. This is a situation not seen since April and, before that, since 2003, during the Second Gulf War.

The arrival of the USS Theodore Roosevelt in the Middle East brings the total number of US aircraft carrier strike groups operating in the region to three. The USS George Washington and the USS George HW Bush are the other two aircraft carriers currently deployed there. That is the biggest surge in US airpower in the Middle East since the Iraq War.

**[ANALYSIS]** The three-carrier deployment provides Trump with maximum military flexibility while simultaneously maintaining pressure on Iran. The scale — not seen since 2003 — signals that full-scale escalation remains an option despite Trump's preference for the "status quo."

**Risk Assessment:** The major near-term risk has shifted from Israeli unilateral action to Iranian desperation. If Iran's mining campaign continues to fail and economic pressure intensifies, Tehran may escalate further — but the US now has three carriers positioned to respond.
