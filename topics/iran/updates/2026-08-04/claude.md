### 7. Iran-Oman Hormuz Negotiations: Technical Exchanges Underway

**[OFFICIAL]** The proposed interim corridor would remain in use until Iran and Oman agree on a permanent replacement for the existing international Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS). **[OFFICIAL]** He stressed that issues related to sovereignty, national security, sovereign rights and maritime services would be fully considered in any eventual agreement. **[REPORTED]** Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi told Iranian state television Oman had proposed a 50-50 arrangement under which the inbound route would be on one country's side and the outbound route on the other's. Instead, Iran proposed placing one shipping lane entirely within Iranian territorial waters and part of the other within Iranian waters.

*Assessment: The Iran-Oman technical track is advancing, but Iran is demanding control over both shipping lanes. This is the core negotiating gap — Iran wants dominance, the US wants "freedom of movement."*
