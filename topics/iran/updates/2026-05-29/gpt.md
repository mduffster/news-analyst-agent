### 2) Trump publicly introduced a **new nuclear/materials handling element** (IAEA involvement + U.S. recovery/destruction claim)
- In an AP “Latest” item, Trump described a plan where the U.S., “in coordination” with Iran and the **IAEA**, would **unearth and destroy** enriched material (“nuclear dust”) buried after earlier strikes. **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8d38406252e36dc0a6759add6e06c9bc?utm_source=openai))  

**What changed / what it means:** This is the first prominent signal (in today’s public messaging) of a potential **technical/verification workstream** being bundled into the MOU narrative. It could help bridge “verification vs. sovereignty” gaps *if real*, but it also raises the risk of **misaligned expectations**: Tehran may accept talks without conceding intrusive recovery operations, while Trump may be positioning this as a deliverable. Watch for rapid clarification from the IAEA and Iranian officials—silence would be meaningful. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8d38406252e36dc0a6759add6e06c9bc?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:** This is an important “two-track” escalation-management development: kinetic expansion on the ground occurring simultaneously with **U.S.-hosted deconfliction/security talks**. It modestly improves prospects for *localized* guardrails in Lebanon, but near-term it also increases “spoiler” risk to any U.S.–Iran pause—images of ground incursions and fatalities make linkage politics harder for Tehran and harder for Washington to sell restraint region-wide.
