*Assessment: [VERIFIED] Despite Trump's claim of "total control," traffic remains at ~10% of normal (~8-11 ships/day vs 100+ pre-war). Iran's conditions for reopening — frozen assets release and regional conflict resolution — remain maximalist and unchanged.*
