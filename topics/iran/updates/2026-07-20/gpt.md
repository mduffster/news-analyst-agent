- The Iran-aligned Houthis said they will **block Saudi shipping at the Bab el‑Mandeb/Red Sea gateway**, framing it as retaliation tied to Saudi actions (incl. claims around Sanaa airport). **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/fd7c4a3911f7eee18251483fc8af768c?utm_source=openai))
