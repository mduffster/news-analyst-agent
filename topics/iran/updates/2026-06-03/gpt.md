### 1) Iran hits **Kuwait’s main airport**, killing 1; Kuwait briefly shuts the airport
- Iranian drones (and/or missiles per some reporting) damaged a passenger terminal at Kuwait International Airport, killing **one** and wounding dozens; Kuwait temporarily suspended operations. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/de2d1814c0f38252bf0383be859c870b?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:** This is a notable **geographic + civilian-infrastructure escalation** in the Gulf. Even if Tehran frames it as pressure on U.S.-aligned states, striking a major civilian airport sharply increases the odds of:  
- tighter Gulf basing/air-defense integration with U.S. forces,

- retaliation cycles that erode the practical meaning of the April 8 ceasefire framework (even if talks continue).
