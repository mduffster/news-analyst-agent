## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Mon, May 11, 2026 (ET)**  
**What changed since Fri, May 8, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) Negotiations: Iran delivered its response; Trump publicly rejected it (“life support” framing)
- **Trump said Iran’s ceasefire/war-ending response was “totally unacceptable,” and described the ceasefire as on “life support.”** **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/0e9067769efea20e9d45e3d43158ad8c?utm_source=openai))  
- **Iran’s reported demands** (via Iranian state/semi-official media as carried by other outlets) included **ending the U.S. naval blockade, lifting sanctions (incl. oil), unfreezing assets, reparations/compensation, and sovereignty-related language over the Strait of Hormuz, plus guarantees against further attacks (including Lebanon).** **[REPORTED]** ([gpb.org](https://www.gpb.org/news/2026/05/10/trump-rejects-irans-latest-response-us-ceasefire-proposal?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** The diplomacy that was “pending a response” on May 8 is now **past the response stage and into a public rejection phase**, which usually **reduces near-term de-escalation odds** and increases incentives for each side to “improve leverage” via maritime pressure and selective strikes. The specific Iranian asks (blockade/sanctions/guarantees/reparations/sovereignty framing) also suggest a **wide gap** with U.S. demands tied to reopening the strait and nuclear constraints (as described in reporting), making a quick interim paper harder unless there’s a quiet backchannel compromise. **[REPORTED]** ([koreajoongangdaily.joins.com](https://koreajoongangdaily.joins.com/news/2026-05-11/national/politics/Trump-rejects-Irans-response-to-US-ceasefire-proposal-as-unacceptable/2588772?utm_source=openai))

### 2) Economic/energy signal: oil >$100 again; U.S. domestic response moves into fuel-price mitigation
- **Brent rose above ~$103** after the rejection headlines, per AP, linking market pricing to expectations that the conflict/closure could drag on. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/bdaa178d57112d9e36dba5e21aa58ded?utm_source=openai))  
- **Trump announced a plan to suspend the federal gas tax**, explicitly tied to high fuel prices from the Iran war / Hormuz disruption. **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/0e9067769efea20e9d45e3d43158ad8c?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** Policy is now reacting to **war-driven energy inflation** as a first-order political constraint. That can cut two ways: it can **increase pressure to find a deal**, but it can also **increase pressure to “force open” flows** through coercive maritime steps if diplomacy stalls.

### 3) Blockade/strait enforcement: CENTCOM details broadened (scale of vessel redirections)
- A CENTCOM-stated accounting (carried by multiple outlets) describes U.S. forces **redirecting dozens of vessels and preventing many tankers from reaching Iranian ports** as part of blockade enforcement. **[OFFICIAL]** ([centcom.mil](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PRESS-RELEASES/Press-Release-View/Article/4480820/us-disables-2-more-vessels-violating-blockade-in-gulf-of-oman/?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** This reinforces that the blockade is not symbolic; it’s an **ongoing high-tempo enforcement regime**. That makes “ceasefire stabilization” more dependent on a negotiated **maritime rules package** (what stops, what continues, and what verification looks like), not just general pledges.
