### 2) The Houthi “blockade” becomes more operational: direct warning to shipping companies
- Reuters (via Al-Monitor) reports the Houthis **emailed shipping companies** warning them **not to load/unload at Saudi ports** and that doing so could result in being targeted **“in any location.”** **[REPORTED]** ([al-monitor.com](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/07/houthis-warn-shipping-companies-avoid-saudi-ports-email-shows?utm_source=openai))  
- Saudi-linked messaging publicly **rejects** the blockade claim and says the coalition has begun **measures to protect vessels** transiting Bab el-Mandeb. **[REPORTED]** ([arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2651670/saudi-arabia?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** Yesterday’s declaration is now being translated into **a compliance/coercion mechanism** aimed at shippers and insurers. That increases the odds of real-world effects (rerouting, higher premiums, fewer port calls) even before a “first interdiction” occurs.
