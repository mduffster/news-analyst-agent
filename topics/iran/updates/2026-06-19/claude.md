### Key Developments Since June 18

#### 1. Hezbollah Attack Kills 4 Israeli Soldiers — Deadliest Since March

**[VERIFIED]** Fighting escalated sharply between Israel and ⁠Hezbollah ⁠in south Lebanon overnight, with four Israeli soldiers killed ⁠in one of the deadliest attacks by the Iran-backed group during this war.

**[VERIFIED]** The escalation followed a Hezbollah attack shortly after midnight on an Israeli tank near Kfar Tebnit, close to the border, which killed all four crew members, including a commander. Several hours later, a drone strike wounded five more Israeli soldiers in the same area.

---

#### 2. Israel Strikes 80+ Targets Across Lebanon — 47 Dead

**[VERIFIED]** The Israeli military killed at least 47 people in Lebanon on Friday, according to the Lebanese health ministry, making it the second deadliest day since hostilities between Israel and Hezbollah flared in early March.

**[VERIFIED]** The strikes, centered on the city of Nabatieh, killed at least 18 people and injured 33, according to Lebanese health officials. Israel Katz, Israel's defense minister, said Israel struck more than 80 targets in Lebanon in retaliation for Hezbollah's attacks.

**[VERIFIED]** The bombardment extended beyond southern Lebanon into the eastern Bekaa Valley, including Baalbek, a historic city and Hezbollah stronghold that had largely escaped this latest round of fighting.

---

#### 3. US-Iran Talks POSTPONED — Vance Trip Canceled

**[OFFICIAL]** Talks between Iran and the U.S. expected to begin in Switzerland Friday were postponed, with the White House blaming logistical issues for the cancellation of JD Vance's trip to Geneva. Officials told The Associated Press and other outlets that Iran had suspended the talks due to the ongoing fighting in Lebanon.

**[VERIFIED]** The announcement followed a report from Al-Mayadeen, a pan-Arab satellite channel that is politically allied with the Iranian-backed Lebanese militant group Hezbollah, that Iran was delaying sending its delegation to Switzerland over Israel's ongoing military campaign in Lebanon.

**[REPORTED]** Vice President JD Vance had been prepared to make an overnight flight Friday to meet with his Iranian counterparts at a mountainside resort in the tiny Swiss village of Obbürgen. His staff and a small pack of journalists had even gathered at Joint Base Andrews outside Washington in anticipation of the trip.

---

#### 4. Israel-Hezbollah Ceasefire RENEWED — Brokered Through Tehran

**[VERIFIED]** Israel and Hezbollah have agreed to a renewed ceasefire starting Friday at 4 p.m. local time, following intensive, behind-the-scenes mediation by the U.S., Qatar, and Iran.

**[VERIFIED]** Recognizing Iran's ability to influence Hezbollah in ways that Lebanon can't, US and Qatar broker truce through Tehran, rather than Beirut.

**[OFFICIAL]** The Israel Defense Forces confirmed the ceasefire deal but added that Israel is prepared "to continue fighting if called upon to do so." There was no immediate comment from Hezbollah or Iran.

---

#### 5. Trump Rehashes Soleimani Grievance Against Netanyahu

**[VERIFIED]** US President Donald Trump suggested Wednesday that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is less justified in criticizing the memorandum of understanding that he signed with Iran because the Israeli premier purportedly pulled out of a joint operation to kill top Iranian general Qassem Soleimani in 2020.

**[OFFICIAL]** "For all of those so-called geniuses who want to show me how smart they are, ask them why didn't they blow up General Soleimani?" Trump challenged.

**[VERIFIED]** For first time in over 2 years, US president brings up purported last-minute Israeli abandonment of op to kill Soleimani, as Jerusalem's policy in Lebanon risks torpedoing MOU with Iran.

---

#### 6. Israeli Poll: 71% Don't Trust Trump on Iran Deal

**[VERIFIED]** A television poll published Thursday found that only a sliver of Israelis feel their country won the Iran war, or trust US President Donald Trump to look out for their interests as he negotiates a deal with the Islamic Republic.

**[VERIFIED]** Most feel that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's conduct hurt Israel's interests in the US-Iran agreement. The numbers mark a stark departure from the robust support Israelis have given Trump for years in poll after poll.

**[OFFICIAL]** Trump said Thursday that he will "most likely" endorse Netanyahu in the fall elections, but wants to see who else is running against him first.

---

#### 7. Commercial Strait Traffic Remains at Zero

**[VERIFIED]** As of June 19, 06:22 UTC, zero commercial vessels have completed their outbound transit today. This grim reality extends a complete cessation of outbound commercial movement that has persisted for the past five days, indicating a severe bottleneck despite recent diplomatic engagements.

**[VERIFIED]** Brent traded around $79 per barrel on Friday and was on track to fall roughly 10% for the week, as investors welcomed improving shipping conditions in the Strait of Hormuz following the implementation of the US-Iran interim peace agreement.

**[OFFICIAL]** The US Central Command announced it had lifted restrictions on traffic to and from Iranian ports and coastal waters, while the Joint Maritime Information Center advised vessels transiting the strait to follow a route closer to Oman's coastline to reduce the risk from mines.

---

#### 8. Iran's Chief Negotiator Issues Warning

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran's chief negotiator, Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf, said on June 19 that upcoming talks with the United States would remain bound by Tehran's "red lines" despite this week's agreement. Qalibaf said Iran would remain committed to protecting its national interests and warned that any attempt to exceed agreed limits would draw a strong response. "If the enemy seeks to be excessive, we have proven that our fingers are on the trigger," he said.

### Emerging Signals

**Iran now holds the leverage:** By conditioning Geneva talks on Lebanon compliance, Tehran has effectively made itself the arbiter of whether the MOU proceeds. This is a significant shift from the US-dominated framing of the initial deal.

**The ceasefire-through-Tehran model:** The US and Qatar brokered this ceasefire through Tehran rather than Beirut—a notable diplomatic innovation that acknowledges Iran's control over Hezbollah but also formalizes Iran's role as guarantor of Lebanese stability.

**Israeli public opinion turning:** 71% distrust of Trump and 11% victory belief are striking numbers for a traditionally pro-Trump population. This could constrain Netanyahu's options in either direction.

**Commercial shipping paralysis persists:** Despite the MOU, blockade lift, and oil flow announcements, zero commercial ships have actually transited outbound in 5+ days. The physical reality lags far behind the diplomatic announcements.
