## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Tue, Aug 11, 2026 (ET)**  
**New since Mon, Aug 10, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) Yemen/Red Sea spillover intensified: **Houthi strike causes first known shipping deaths in the current bout**
- Yemen’s internationally recognized government said **Houthis hit a commercial vessel in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait with ballistic missiles, killing four** (reported as **three Pakistani nationals and an Indonesian** in one AP account; another AP writeup describes the dead slightly differently) and **wounding a Yemeni rescuer** after a follow-on strike on rescue teams. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/84ee037681e5455587a4273eb777709e))  
- AP frames this as occurring amid a **renewed focus on the Red Sea/Bab el-Mandeb** because **Hormuz remains effectively shut**, raising the premium on alternate routes—especially for Saudi oil shipments. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/91e4efdfe1ac035b2065127550377289))  

**What changed / what it means:** This is a material escalation on the maritime/proxy track: a shift from “threats/harassment/attacks” to **confirmed fatalities tied to shipping**, which tends to harden insurance/escort dynamics and increases the odds of retaliatory cycles around chokepoints beyond Hormuz.

### 2) Hormuz diplomacy: public positions **harden**, and the issue is increasingly framed as **“unconditional reopening” vs “conditions-first”**
- Iran’s FM **Abbas Araghchi** reiterated that Iran **won’t consider reopening the Strait of Hormuz until U.S. conditions are met** (including ending the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports and other demands), while also portraying Iran as having proven resilience in the war. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/91e4efdfe1ac035b2065127550377289))  
- A parallel diplomatic signal: Araghchi described a call with Germany’s FM where Iran emphasized security in Hormuz requires an end to U.S. “aggressive actions,” while Germany’s FM publicly emphasized Hormuz **“needs to be opened unconditionally.”** **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/84ee037681e5455587a4273eb777709e))  

**What changed / what it means:** Compared with yesterday’s “pending Oman-channel text + added Iranian terms” posture, today adds **more explicit public entrenchment**: Iran is messaging **conditions-first**, while at least one key European voice is signaling **unconditional** reopening. That widens the “face-saving gap” and makes near-term implementation less likely unless there’s a quiet sequencing compromise.

---

### 3) Energy-market pressure: oil/gasoline uptick tied to uncertainty on Hormuz timeline
- AP reports **renewed oil-price uncertainty** around when Hormuz reopens, with **Brent ~\$87.61** and **WTI ~\$82.19** early Tuesday, and **AAA average regular gasoline at \$4.01**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/84ee037681e5455587a4273eb777709e))  

**What changed / what it means:** The conflict’s “economic front” is reasserting itself. If the Bab el-Mandeb threat persists while Hormuz stays shut, the market impact becomes more structurally linked to **multi-chokepoint risk**, not just Hormuz bargaining.
