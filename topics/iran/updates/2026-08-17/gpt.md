### 1) Hormuz: Iran says it’s near-finalizing an Oman-mediated shipping “plan” (route map + joint statement)
- Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson said **Iran and Oman have reached an “understanding” on a “map of the transit route”** for ships through the Strait of Hormuz and are working to finalize details in a **joint statement**. **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/53ae10812e472a4e5bb228b9f318a5fc))  
- AP reports this is tied to the broader standoff: the U.S. is seeking an acceptable arrangement to open the strait **before** ending its blockade of Iranian ports. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/53ae10812e472a4e5bb228b9f318a5fc))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
- This is the first concrete *diplomatic/process* movement in several days that points toward a **managed de-escalation mechanism** for Hormuz (even if limited/conditional). If it progresses from “understanding” to an announced framework, it could reduce the probability of repeated tanker strikes becoming the dominant escalation engine. The key uncertainty is whether the “route map” is actually implementable (enforcement, inspection, escorts, guarantees) and whether the U.S. accepts it.
