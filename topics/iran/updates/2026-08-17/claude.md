### 3. Iran-Oman Reach Hormuz Transit Agreement

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said "an understanding has been reached" regarding transit in the Strait of Hormuz. Details reportedly suggest that ships are set to enter the agreed-upon transit route near Iran's shores and exit close to Oman, while neither country will seek tolls for transit during an interim period.

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran said Monday it has reached an agreement with Oman over a plan for ships to transit the Strait of Hormuz, which runs between the two countries, and the sides are now working to finalize details for a joint statement. Iranian Foreign Affairs spokesperson Esmail Baghaei told reporters in Tehran that "an understanding has been reached regarding the map of the transit route."

**[VERIFIED]** Details that have previously emerged suggest the deal would reopen the strait with ships entering through a route close to Iran and exiting through a route close to Oman. Ships would transit without paying fees or tolls during the interim period.

**[UNCONFIRMED]** The U.S. has sought an acceptable deal on opening the strait before ending its blockade of Iranian ports and it was not clear whether this would meet Washington's expectations.

*Assessment: This represents progress on the Iran-Oman bilateral track, but it's explicitly decoupled from US-Iran negotiations. Trump's threat to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way" appears directly targeted at this Iran-Oman arrangement. The toll-free interim period suggests both sides want implementation before tackling the more contentious issue of fees.*

**[REPORTED]** Barzani asked Vahidi if he was aligned with the Iranian negotiators. The Iranian general said he was. "I fully support them and this is also the position of the IRGC. We prefer to solve this crisis through negotiations," Vahidi said, according to a source with direct knowledge.

*Assessment: The conflicting claims about the IRGC backchannel create uncertainty about the state of talks.
