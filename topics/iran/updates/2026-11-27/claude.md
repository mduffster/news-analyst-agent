### **TRUMP CLAIMS "VICTORY AT HAND" — TARGETS PICKAXE MOUNTAIN** [OFFICIAL]

President Trump stated that he can end the war with Iran "whenever I want." Trump made the remarks while speaking by phone with US service members stationed in the Middle East from his Mar-a-Lago estate in Florida in celebration of Thanksgiving. Trump told the troops, "You will continue your deployment," emphasizing, "We are on the verge of victory."

In response to reporters' questions about whether he had spoken with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, Trump answered that he had. He then added, "We are trying to give them (Iran) a chance to act wisely." He stated, "We will definitely strike Pickaxe Mountain. However, we have not seen any actual activity there."

Pickaxe Mountain, also known as Kuh-e Kolang Gaz La or Mount Kolang, is a deeply buried facility near Iran's Natanz nuclear complex in Isfahan province. The site has attracted growing attention from US and Israeli officials because of its underground tunnel network and its potential connection to Iran's nuclear programme.

**[ANALYSIS]** Trump's Thanksgiving messaging reinforces the "victory through pressure" framing while maintaining threat posture. The Pickaxe Mountain statement is conditional — he says the US would "absolutely" strike it if resuming operations, but acknowledges no current activity there. This suggests the administration is positioning for either negotiations or future escalation, while keeping options open through January.

### **SAUDI AIR DEFENSE PERFORMANCE REVEALED — 90% INTERCEPT RATE** [VERIFIED]

The kingdom's air defence network destroyed 252 of at least 278 hostile munitions fired from Yemen and Iraq.

### **IRAN CONFIRMS MEDIATION CONTINUES — DEMANDS UNCHANGED** [OFFICIAL]

Iran's state-run Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA) reported Araghchi "acknowledged the ongoing mediation efforts by Qatar and Pakistan," and said "proposals put forward by the two countries are currently under review." Qatar's Al-Ansari said, "What I can confirm is that both sides are engaging in the mediation as we speak right now."

Araghchi said, "We are examining ideas that could open new paths, but we insist on implementing our demands related to reopening the Strait of Hormuz." The demands Iran conveyed to Washington include lifting what Tehran calls the maritime blockade, guaranteeing safe passage for commercial vessels and oil tankers through the Strait of Hormuz, removing unilateral sanctions, granting exemptions for oil exports and releasing frozen Iranian assets. The demands also include keeping nuclear materials inside Iran and ending the war.

Analysts note that the mediation is ongoing but that Iran's stated demands remain far from Washington's position, and that no deal appears imminent.

**[ANALYSIS]** This follows Kushner's confirmation of direct talks. Iran's "seven demands" position remains maximalist, but the acknowledgment of active mediation suggests both sides are keeping channels open. Combined with Trump's "giving them a chance to be smart" framing, there's a diplomatic track running parallel to military pressure — though no breakthrough appears imminent.
