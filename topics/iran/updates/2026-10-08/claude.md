### **HORMUZ TRANSITS HIT TWO-MONTH LOW** [VERIFIED]

Oil prices climbed Thursday on continued worries about global supply following a record number of tanker attacks that sent crossings through the Strait of Hormuz to a two-month low. Just seven tankers transited the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, less than half the seven-day average and the lowest number since July 23, preliminary data from Kpler showed.

Despite the rising number of so-called "dark" transits, there have been a record number of attacks on commercial vessels, with 10 tankers struck in the strait between September 28 and October 4, well above the previous weekly high of six. The rise in attacks comes even as more crude has been flowing out of the Gulf, highlighting the ongoing risks to crew and cargoes.

"There is a clear tug-of-war at the moment between improving supply from the region and lingering threats to supply," commodities strategists at ING wrote.

### **PUTIN MEETS PEZESHKIAN IN TURKMENISTAN** [VERIFIED]

Although the meeting with Masoud Pezeshkian was originally scheduled for Friday, the leaders of the two countries decided to hold it today. ### **HOUTHIS STRIKE RIYADH AIRPORT AGAIN** [VERIFIED]

Yemen's Houthis said on Thursday they targeted King Khalid International Airport in the Saudi capital Riyadh with a missile for a second time, as the group escalated its strikes on airports across the neighbouring kingdom. "Our armed forces targeted King Khalid Airport in Riyadh at midday with a ballistic missile that hit its target," said the Houthis' military spokesman Yahya Saree.

The Saudi-led coalition fighting the group said earlier on Thursday it had intercepted two missiles fired at the kingdom's capital. Saudi Arabia's civil defence agency reported "material damage" to buildings from falling shrapnel after the interceptions. Riyadh has not disclosed any further information on damage or casualties.

Several explosions echoed across Riyadh on Thursday morning, witnesses said. Diplomats were instructed to shelter in place in their respective embassies. Meanwhile, King Khalid Airport in Riyadh asked departing passengers to contact their airlines before coming to the airport.

**Cumulative airport attack toll:** Saudi Arabia's Abha International Airport and King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh were targeted in two separate attacks, claimed by the Houthis on Tuesday and Wednesday. At least three civilians were killed and 36 injured, according to a statement from the Saudi General Authority of Civil Aviation.

### **OPERATION YEMEN DAWN: HEAVY CASUALTIES REPORTED** [VERIFIED]

The spokesperson for the internationally recognised government's military said in a social media post early on Thursday it had carried out 2,103 "precise targeting operations" against Houthi military positions across several fronts as part of the ongoing "Dawn of Yemen" campaign.

On October 6, the PLC reported that operations in Taiz the day prior killed 100 Houthis and injured 150 others. Houthi sources reported that 160 fighters were killed in the past 24 hours, while the PLC reported that 115 soldiers were killed.

**UN casualty figures:** The UN World Health Organization reported more than 5,100 casualties since 6 August, including 959 deaths, according to its latest situation report.

Almost 1,000 people have been killed in the latest fighting, with over 200,000 displaced, according to the United Nations.
