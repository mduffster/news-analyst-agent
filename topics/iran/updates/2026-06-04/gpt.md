### 1) **IAEA says it cannot account for Iran’s enriched-uranium stockpile (location/composition) and can’t carry out safeguards**
- The IAEA circulated a report saying it **“cannot provide any information”** on the **size/composition/whereabouts** of Iran’s enriched uranium stockpile or whether enrichment activities have been suspended, and that it is **unable to discharge safeguards responsibilities**. It notes **Bushehr** was inspected **June 1–3** (and appears to be the only site visited since the last report in February). **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/28da35ab9a372494337a471fb0fa6048?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:**  
- This is a **nuclear-risk escalation in the information domain**: even if kinetic exchanges stay “limited,” reduced visibility raises (a) worst-case planning in Washington/Jerusalem, (b) the chance of preemptive or coercive strikes justified by uncertainty, and (c) pressure on any deal framework to include **immediate, verifiable re-access**—not just promises on enrichment levels.

### 2) **Kuwait releases video/footage of the airport strike; Iran/IRGC deny responsibility and blame Patriot malfunction**
- Multiple outlets report **released surveillance/footage** of the strike on Kuwait International Airport facilities. **[REPORTED]** ([theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/world/video/2026/jun/04/surveillance-footage-shows-moment-of-drone-attack-on-kuwait-airport-video?utm_source=openai))  
- In parallel, Iranian/IRGC-linked messaging **denies firing at the airport** and claims damage came from a **malfunctioning U.S.-made Patriot interceptor**. **[OFFICIAL]** (Iran/IRGC claim) ([aa.com.tr](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/iran-says-damage-at-kuwait-airport-terminal-caused-by-us-patriot-missile-malfunction/3955764?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:**  
- **Attribution warfare** is now central: Kuwait’s release of footage is meant to **lock in blame** publicly and diplomatically, while Tehran’s Patriot-malfunction narrative aims to (a) avoid widening the coalition against Iran and (b) shift liability onto U.S./Gulf air defenses.  
- Expect **higher Gulf partner demand** for U.S. air-defense integration *and* stricter basing/ROE discussions—because civilian-infrastructure strikes create domestic political pressure inside Gulf states.
