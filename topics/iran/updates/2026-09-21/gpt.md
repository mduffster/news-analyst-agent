**What changed / what it means**
- **Change:** A clearer public **deterrence/threat framing** tied explicitly to widening target sets and theaters. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/82714681bde58c6525c2d7a1bc587b25))  
- **Meaning:** Raises the odds that Iran (or aligned actors) tries to impose costs **outside the most expected lanes** (e.g., additional maritime chokepoints, partner territory, or different target categories), increasing deconfliction burden on the U.S. and regional states.

### 3) Negotiation track: Iran relays conditions for re-engaging in talks via mediators
- Reuters reporting (carried by GMA News) says Iran, via security chief Mohsen Rezaei, conveyed conditions to mediators for restarting negotiations: **end the war “on all fronts,” unfreeze Iranian funds, and end the naval blockade**, with consultations continuing with **Qatari and Pakistani mediators**, and Tehran awaiting a response from President Trump. **[REPORTED]** ([gmanetwork.com](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/1002988/iran-says-it-has-conveyed-conditions-to-re-engage-in-talks-end-war-with-us/story/))

**What changed / what it means**
- **Change:** More explicit **Iranian “terms list”** for talks, suggesting the mediation channel is active enough to pass structured demands. **[REPORTED]** ([gmanetwork.com](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/1002988/iran-says-it-has-conveyed-conditions-to-re-engage-in-talks-end-war-with-us/story/))  
- **Meaning:** This is *not* a breakthrough, but it modestly improves the near-term odds of a **bounded deconfliction package** (maritime rules + partial financial steps) *if* Washington sees leverage in trading narrow concessions for reduced Hormuz risk. Conversely, the ask to end the war “on all fronts” is likely too sweeping, implying continued stalemate unless bridged by phased steps.
