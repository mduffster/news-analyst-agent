### 1) Red Sea / Bab el‑Mandeb: Houthis expand from Mayun to the **Hanish island chain**
- Yemen’s Houthis **seized Greater and Lesser Hanish islands** (north of Bab el‑Mandeb), per AP citing Yemeni government and Houthi officials. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b95a5a6c73a26bbbb1caddcbf365ca62))  
- AP also reports Yemeni government forces are attempting to respond with **airstrikes on Houthi positions** around Mokha/Dhubab/Taiz. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8c18d82c109a8ea91347ce53c0096c53))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
- **Change:** The Houthi posture is no longer just “hold Mayun/Perim + Mokha.” It is **widening into an island-network strategy** that can support surveillance, staging, and harassment along Red Sea approaches—not just the strait itself. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b95a5a6c73a26bbbb1caddcbf365ca62))  
- **Meaning:** This makes “two‑chokepoint stress” (Hormuz + Bab el‑Mandeb) more structurally durable. Even if coalition pressure disrupts Mayun, the Hanish positions add **redundancy** and complicate rollback. Net: higher probability that maritime security becomes a **continuous campaign requirement**, not a surge operation.

### 2) Saudi export squeeze deepens: East‑West pipeline damage now framed as **weeks-long**
- AP reports repairs to the Saudi **East‑West (Petroline) pipeline** will take **~3–5 weeks** and the line will be **mostly out of service** (possibly partially operating during repairs), per two officials briefed on the matter. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8c18d82c109a8ea91347ce53c0096c53))  
- Context in the same reporting: the pipeline had become more critical because the Iran war has constrained the Gulf exit (Hormuz), forcing more dependence on Red Sea export routes—now threatened by Houthi gains. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8c18d82c109a8ea91347ce53c0096c53))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
- **Change:** This is a **time-horizon update**: the disruption isn’t a short-lived shock; it’s a multi‑week constraint. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8c18d82c109a8ea91347ce53c0096c53))  
- **Meaning:** The Saudi “export optionality” problem is now acute: **(a)** Gulf route constrained, **(b)** Red Sea route threatened, **(c)** cross‑kingdom bypass impaired. That increases incentives for Riyadh to press the U.S. for expanded action against Houthi launch infrastructure and/or for intensified counter‑UAS efforts.
