## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Wed, July 1, 2026 (ET)**  
**New since Tue, June 30, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) Doha diplomacy: “indirect technical talks” are now actually underway (still *no* direct U.S.–Iran meetings)
- **Indirect technical talks between U.S. and Iranian teams are underway in Doha**, with **Qatar and Pakistan mediating**, per a source cited by Reuters. **[REPORTED]** ([rferl.org](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-war-us-hormuz-oil-blockade-gulf-israel/33640284.html?lbis=461226))  
- **Witkoff and Kushner are not participating in the technical talks**; they met Qatari leadership June 30 to set the stage. **[REPORTED]** ([rferl.org](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-war-us-hormuz-oil-blockade-gulf-israel/33640284.html?lbis=461226))  
- **Iran continues to publicly deny any direct negotiations “at any level” in coming days**, reinforcing the “mediated troubleshooting” format rather than leader-level bargaining. **[OFFICIAL]** ([arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2649234/amp))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
Yesterday’s uncertainty about whether Doha would produce anything concrete has narrowed: **there is now an active technical channel**. That modestly improves odds of near-term “micro-deconfliction” (shipping lanes, procedures, verification), but because it remains **indirect and technical**, it’s still vulnerable to any new maritime incident or proxy escalation outpacing the talks.

---

### 2) Strait of Hormuz: a ship running aground becomes a “route control” messaging event for Tehran
- **AP reports a container ship ran aground in the Strait of Hormuz**; Iranian state TV emphasized it was using a route “not approved by Iran” and said shippers should follow IRGC instructions. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/de0729197bc7b9d3ee9e543d94c18fbe))  
- AP frames this as Iran **underscoring its claim to control routing** in what much of the world treats as an international waterway—directly tied to the ongoing dispute over **fees/routes/authority** in the interim arrangements. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/de0729197bc7b9d3ee9e543d94c18fbe))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
Even if this is a navigational incident rather than an attack, it’s operationally and politically relevant: Iran is using **compliance-with-Iran-routing** as the public test of “safe passage.” That increases the risk that a future “noncompliant” transit—especially under escort—turns into a coercive interception or exchange, even while Doha talks proceed.
