## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**Report date:** **Fri, Nov 20, 2026 (ET)**  
**Update window vs prior report:** **since Thu, Nov 19, 2026 (ET)**

### What’s new / what changed (since Nov 19)

1) **Diplomacy channel: Witkoff & Kushner met Qatari mediators (Berlin) on Iran talks**
- **What changed:** Axios reports Trump envoys **Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner** met **senior Qatari officials in Berlin (Thu, Nov 19)** to discuss Qatar’s mediation to **resume a ceasefire, restart nuclear negotiations, and prevent a return to war**. **[REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/20/witkoff-kushner-meet-qatari-mediators-iran))  
- **What it means:** This is the first concrete “process signal” in days that the White House is actively **working a negotiation track in parallel** with the blockade/limited-strike posture. It doesn’t eliminate escalation risk, but it **raises the probability of a managed freeze/partial de-escalation** over the next 2–12 months (even if messy and incomplete).

2) **Escalation planning: CBS says U.S. + Israel are considering a new short-term assault; CBW rhetoric is now on-record**
- **What changed:** CBS reports **U.S. and Israel are considering launching a new assault on Iran “in the short term,”** citing sources familiar; it also reports Trump said Iran may be seeking **chemical/biological** capabilities (“gases… bio”) **without providing evidence**, and Iran’s foreign ministry pushed back (WMD-2003 Iraq analogy). **[REPORTED]** ([cbsnews.com](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/iran-war-us-israel-considering-new-assault-sources-say/?intcid=CNI-00-10aaa3a))  
- **What it means:** Compared with yesterday’s Axios “considering strikes” reporting, today adds:
  - a second major outlet echoing **near-term strike consideration** (still source-based), and
  - a **notable escalation in messaging**: the CBW angle is now tied to **Trump’s own public remarks** (even if unsubstantiated).  
  Net: tail risk of a “punctuated escalation” in the next 0–60 days **ticks up**, even as diplomacy signals pull in the opposite direction.

3) **Iraq flashpoint: Jurf al‑Sakhar move is now implemented (not just negotiated)**
- **What changed:** AP reports Iraqi security forces **set up a command headquarters Friday** near **Jurf al‑Sakhar** to assert control in the Kataib Hezbollah stronghold; the deal reportedly involves **PMF participation** and a **timeline for Kataib Hezbollah withdrawal within a month**, with Quds Force commander **Esmail Qaani** cited as facilitating. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/saudi-yemen-war-iran-iraq-november-20-2026-16c39c487a23b53b73c5eabb7f0fd57a))  
- **What it means:** This reduces immediate uncertainty about whether Baghdad can “enter at all” without a firefight; however, the inclusion of PMF forces and a one-month withdrawal window means this is **stabilization-by-compromise**, not a clean rollback. Near-term risk of Iraq acting as the next sudden escalation trigger is **slightly lower**, but watch for **spoilers** (attacks shifting elsewhere).

4) **Oil/shipping datapoint: exports reportedly ~80% of prewar levels; early-November dip linked to attacks & pipeline disruption**
- **What changed:** AP cites analysts/Rystad Energy that exports are running around **~80% of prewar** with a **~20% decline in the first two weeks of Nov vs Oct**, attributing it to **Iranian attacks discouraging tankers** and **reduced Saudi pipeline flows to Yanbu after attacks**. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/saudi-yemen-war-iran-iraq-november-20-2026-16c39c487a23b53b73c5eabb7f0fd57a))  
- **What it means:** This supports a “limbo” picture: the blockade/attacks are **meaningfully disruptive but not fully choking flows**. That tends to prolong the conflict dynamics (pressure without decision), sustaining incentives for intermittent harassment/retaliation rather than rapid closure.

Net effect is a **small increase** in short-horizon escalation risk, but also a **modest increase** in the odds of an eventual managed off-ramp over the 2–12 month horizon.
