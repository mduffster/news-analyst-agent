### **TRUMP: WAR WITH IRAN "EFFECTIVELY OVER"** [VERIFIED]

United States President Donald Trump has signalled that he considers the US-Israeli war against Iran "effectively" at an end, after meeting with mediators from Qatar. In a surprise briefing on Monday, Trump reiterated his position that the US had destroyed Iran's military. But his remarks suggested he faced pressure from allies in the Middle East to bring hostilities to a close. "Iran, militarily, is effectively over. We're just keeping it the status quo," he said. "We're doing that really at the behest of countries that are their neighbours."

Trump said, however, that the U.S. had identified infrastructure targets, including 38 bridges, that it would strike if military action resumes.

Trump added that Iran has been "decapitated" but said he is still looking to make sure there's a long-term solution to ensuring that Iran's nuclear program is defanged. The president offered some of his most definitive comments about moving away from his threat to resume strikes on Iran after the U.S. midterm elections passed.

**[ANALYSIS]** Trump's "effectively over" declaration is his clearest signal yet that he does not intend to resume major combat operations. This represents a significant rhetorical shift from pre-midterm "finish the job" threats. However, the "locked and loaded" hedge and 38 targets comment preserve escalation options.

Massie, a Kentucky Republican, read the impeachment resolution from the House floor just hours after it opened for the first day of sessions after the midterms. It largely focuses on the war in Iran but also includes references to Hegseth's role in carrying out military actions in Yemen and Venezuela.

President Donald Trump lashed out at Massie personally just hours after the congressman read the resolution, calling him a "loser" who "has a lot of hatred in his heart."

**[ANALYSIS]** Massie's privileged resolution forces GOP leadership into an uncomfortable vote by Wednesday. With Democrats likely unified and only 3 GOP defections needed, this creates genuine risk for Hegseth. Even if it fails, the vote forces vulnerable Republicans on record defending the war.

### **IRGC ATTACKS 3 MORE TANKERS; NAVIG8 MESSI IDENTIFIED** [VERIFIED — WITH INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION]

On Saturday, at least two tankers were attacked in the Strait of Hormuz, including one that caught fire. The first vessel was transiting about eight nautical miles off the coast of Khasab, on Oman's Musandam Peninsula, when it caught fire.

The Navig8 Messi, an oil products tanker indirectly owned by UAE-based ADNOC L&S, has been identified as the vessel hit by the IRGC northwest of Khasab, Oman. The ship is on fire. IRGC drones also attacked it on August 13 during an outbound transit of the Strait of Hormuz. The ship was hit once previously in August, repaired, and returned to the same trade lane.

Satellite images depicted smoke rising from the Strait. Authorities from the United Kingdom had confirmed incidents with two tankers crossing the Strait this morning, but announced that no casualties were sustained by any of the crew.

**[ANALYSIS]** Unlike prior IRGC claims, these attacks have partial independent confirmation (UKMTO, OSINT identification of vessel). The repeat targeting of Navig8 Messi shows Iran's pattern of attacking ships that return to service. Tanker operators will face increased pressure to stay out.

### **IRAN LIFTS 2,000KM MISSILE RANGE LIMIT** [OFFICIAL]

Hossein Mohebbi confirmed that Iran was now abandoning its previous 2,000-kilometer, or roughly 1,240-mile, ballistic missile-range restriction, according to The Associated Press. "That restriction has been lifted for us," Mohebbi clarified in the wake of the task force announcement.

The announcement does not mean Iran already possesses missiles capable of exceeding the previous limit. There is currently no publicly confirmed evidence that Tehran has operational ballistic missiles with a range greater than 2,000 kilometers. However, the policy change potentially removes an important constraint on the country's future missile development.

**[ANALYSIS]** This is a symbolic escalation signal timed to coincide with CENTCOM's land blockade announcement. Iran is signaling capacity to strike beyond the Middle East. Whether they have such missiles is unclear, but the policy shift matters for future negotiations.

### **CENTCOM ESTABLISHES "TASK FORCE IRON GATE" FOR LAND BLOCKADE** [VERIFIED]

US Central Command (CENTCOM) announced on Saturday the creation of a new task force aimed at disrupting Iran's movement of advanced weapons, sanctioned materials and other shipments across its land borders and the Caspian Sea. CENTCOM commander Admiral Brad Cooper ordered the establishment of Task Force Iron Gate, a team of approximately 20 military and civilian personnel based in Tampa, Florida.

The United States military's Central Command (CENTCOM) has announced a plan to block Iran's land-based supply routes, expanding its months-long naval blockade of Iranian ports in the Strait of Hormuz. CENTCOM announced on Saturday that it had established a new task force "to prevent Iran from using ground routes to transport advanced weapons and other materials".

CENTCOM said the US naval blockade had prevented Iran from exporting more than $25 billion worth of crude oil in recent months.

**[ANALYSIS]** This represents an escalation of economic pressure even as Trump signals de-escalation rhetorically. The blockade is expanding, not contracting. Iran has developed overland routes through Central Asia and the Caspian; this task force aims to close that gap.

Gadi Eisenkot formally received the mandate on Sunday to form the next Israeli governing coalition, and vowed to pursue "creative and unconventional" pathways to assembling what he termed a "national emergency government."

By law, Eisenkot will now have 28 days to form a government, and the president may grant an extension of up to 14 additional days if necessary.

Eisenkot has a mandate and a parliamentary majority on paper, but not yet a governing coalition. Eisenkot is pushing to form what he calls a "national emergency government" and to swear in a minority government as soon as possible.

Netanyahu has seized on the issue, claiming in a recorded address on November 7 that some intended "to hurriedly swear in a government with supporters of terrorism".

**[ANALYSIS]** Eisenkot now has until December 6 (plus potential 14-day extension) to form a coalition. His "creative and unconventional" language suggests he may pursue a minority government with Arab party external support. Netanyahu's rhetoric about "terrorism supporters" signals he will fight this.

**[ANALYSIS]** Iran is publicly rejecting even the framing of "negotiations." Trump's "effectively over" declaration and Rezaei's "fake news" tweet show a massive gap in how both sides characterize the situation. This mismatch suggests diplomatic progress is being overstated.
