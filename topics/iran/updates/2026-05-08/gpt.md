## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**As of Fri, May 8, 2026 (ET)**  
**What changed since Thu, May 7, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) Ceasefire erosion became more explicit: U.S.–Iran exchanged fire around Hormuz; UAE reports being targeted
- **U.S. military said it intercepted Iranian attacks on three U.S. Navy ships in the Strait of Hormuz and struck Iranian military facilities “responsible” for the attacks.** **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/6490db55a65880a61a6233eff7acc68b))  
- **UAE reported it faced an Iranian missile/drone attack** (UAE MoD: two ballistic missiles + three drones engaged; **three wounded**). **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/6490db55a65880a61a6233eff7acc68b))  

**What it means:** This is a shift from “maritime governance/coercion” (vetting/taxing regimes) to **kinetic tit-for-tat** involving U.S. naval units and **Gulf-state air defense**, which raises the odds of miscalculation and regional spillover even if negotiators keep talking.

---

### 2) The blockade line hardened: U.S. says it disabled **two more** Iran-flagged tankers
- AP reports the **U.S. fired on and disabled two additional Iran-flagged oil tankers** allegedly trying to breach the American blockade of Iran’s ports. **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/883a1f388f2ae8b99d70ea3a4f177525?utm_source=openai))  
- Iran, via state-media-carried statements, **accused the U.S. of ceasefire violations** tied to these tanker actions. **[OFFICIAL]** ([internazionale.it](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/05/07/iran-accuses-us-of-violating-ceasefire-with-new-attacks?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** The U.S. is signaling it will **enforce the blockade operationally**, not just declare it. That makes any interim “one-page” understanding harder to stabilize unless it **explicitly** addresses (a) what “reopening the strait” means in practice and (b) what enforcement actions stop/continue.

---

### 3) Lebanon lane: Israel–Hezbollah exchanges continued after the Beirut strike
- **Israeli strikes in southern Lebanon killed at least five** (per Lebanon’s Health Ministry reporting cited by AP); Hezbollah fired rockets into northern Israel that reportedly caused no casualties. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/8feffe888b03e6fb8031ecbb9dfca1ae))  

**What it means:** This keeps the Israel–Hezbollah front “warm” enough to **force linkage** into U.S. diplomacy (and into Iranian escalation calculations), even if neither side is choosing full escalation today.

---

### 4) A diplomatic “container” is taking shape: Israel–Lebanon talks scheduled May 14–15 in Washington; Iran response timing flagged
- AP reports **direct Israel–Lebanon talks are scheduled to resume May 14–15 in Washington**, per a U.S. official. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/6490db55a65880a61a6233eff7acc68b))  
- AP also reports Secretary of State **Marco Rubio expected to hear from Iran “later Friday”** on the negotiation track. **[OFFICIAL]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/6490db55a65880a61a6233eff7acc68b))  

**What it means:** Washington appears to be trying to **parallel-process** two deconfliction tracks (Iran/Hormuz and Israel/Lebanon). That can reduce cross-front “spoiler” risk *if* the tracks produce enforceable understandings quickly; but scheduling talks doesn’t yet change battlefield incentives.
