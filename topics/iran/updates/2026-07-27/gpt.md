- **Multiple credible reports indicate a pause in U.S. strikes and corresponding Iranian restraint**, with mediators (notably **Qatar and Pakistan**) pushing to restore/bridge an interim ceasefire framework; Iran says messages may be conveyed via mediators but **no direct talks**. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/d57e675a7be6dbdd34561909ced240d0?utm_source=openai))

### 3) Kuwait/Iraq land link stabilizes: Abdali crossing reopens after drone attack closure
- **Reuters reporting says the main Kuwait–Iraq land crossing (Abdali) has reopened** after being temporarily closed due to the drone attack that caused material damage but no casualties. **[VERIFIED]** (single Reuters report but widely reliable; aligns with prior Kuwait army account) ([theprint.in](https://theprint.in/world/main-land-crossing-between-kuwait-and-iraq-reopens-iraqs-state-news-agency-says/2997168/?utm_source=openai))  
**What it means:** This is a *small but real* containment datapoint: Gulf spillover remains a risk, but the reopening suggests **no immediate spiral** around the incident—at least at the border/logistics level.

**What it means:** This strengthens diplomatic cover for sustained escorts/defensive maritime operations and related pressure tools. Even if U.S.–Iran strikes pause, the **proxy-maritime front** can keep the region in a persistent “security campaign” posture.
