## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict

**What changed since Fri, May 22, 2026 (ET)**

### 1) The “deal to end the war” messaging moved from *slight progress* → *near-term signing expectations* (but with visible hedges)
- **Trump** publicly claimed an agreement is **“largely negotiated”** / will be finalized **“shortly”** (over the weekend), and on **May 25** said negotiations are **“proceeding nicely,”** while again warning fighting could resume if no deal is reached. **[OFFICIAL]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/23/us-iran-trump-deal-war?utm_source=openai))  
- **White House framing (Axios)**: officials say a deal could take **“days”** and admit it’s **not finalized** and could still **fall apart**, pointing to Iran’s slower decision system; they also say **Netanyahu’s team is involved** in the process. **[REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/24/iran-deal-white-house-delay-days-trump?utm_source=openai))  
- **AP “what we know/don’t know”** underscores that key implementation timing and mechanics remain unclear—i.e., there’s still a gap between political announcements and an enforceable instrument. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b1659232611edc10808612e30647c17d?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** The center of gravity shifted toward a **near-term ceasefire extension / deal announcement window** (days, not weeks). But the explicit “could fall apart” and “opaque decision-making” caveats signal this is still a **fragile, late-stage negotiation** where spoilers or last-minute conditions could flip the trajectory quickly.

### 2) Israel–U.S. alignment: a clearer “freedom to act” red line is now in the open
- **Reuters (via Internazionale)** reports **Netanyahu told Trump** Israel must remain **free to act against threats in Lebanon** during the emerging U.S.–Iran deal process. **[REPORTED]** ([internazionale.it](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/05/24/netanyahu-told-trump-israel-will-remain-free-to-act-against-threats-israeli-source-says?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** This is a concrete indicator of a **two-track reality**: even if Washington and Tehran lock in a ceasefire extension, Israel may insist on **continued operational latitude** (especially against Hezbollah/Lebanon). That raises the risk that *post-deal* incidents could still trigger **retaliatory spirals** even without a formal breakdown of the U.S.–Iran channel.

### 3) Pakistan mediator lane: trip appears to have produced “productive” engagements, but not a signed outcome yet
- Reporting indicates **Munir left Tehran after meetings** aimed at pushing the deal “across the line,” with Pakistan’s military describing the visit as **productive** in the context of mediation. **[OFFICIAL]** (Pakistan military statement as reported) ([washingtonpost.com](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/05/23/us-seeks-iran-ceasefire-extension-war-threats-loom//?utm_source=openai))  

**What it means:** Pakistan’s role looks less like a one-off conduit and more like an **active shuttle mediator**. Still, the absence (so far) of a signed text suggests remaining gaps are **political-authorization** gaps (Tehran decision chain) and/or **sequencing/guarantees** issues.
