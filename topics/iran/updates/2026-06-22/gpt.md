- Vice President **JD Vance held lengthy talks with senior Iranian officials in Switzerland**, saying they set a “good foundation” for a final deal. **[OFFICIAL/REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/4bbde727c7095c4ad9da0285ca79f1e1?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:**  
Friday’s “talks didn’t start” setback now looks like a **short delay rather than a derailment**. The bigger signal is *agenda*: Lebanon enforcement mechanisms are being treated as a **core implementation track**, not a side issue—suggesting Tehran (and Washington) still view Lebanon as the main tripwire that can collapse the broader process.

### 2) **IAEA inspectors: Vance says Iran will allow UN nuclear inspectors back in**
- Vance announced Iran agreed to **invite IAEA inspectors back** into the country, per Axios. **[OFFICIAL/REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/22/iran-un-inspectors-vance?utm_source=openai))  

**What changed / what it means:**  
If executed, this is the most concrete **verifiability upgrade** since the MOU and would materially improve the odds of a durable nuclear track (and reduce pressure for renewed kinetic “enforcement” strikes). Caveat: this is an announced commitment; the key next marker is **timing + access scope** (sites, sampling, monitoring continuity).
