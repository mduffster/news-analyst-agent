## Key Developments (Past 24 Hours)

### **IRAN ATTACKS KUWAIT FOR FIRST TIME SINCE SEPTEMBER** [VERIFIED]

Iran on Wednesday launched apparent retaliatory strikes on a U.S. military base in Kuwait and several sites in Bahrain, just hours after the U.S. bombed an Iranian missile site in the Strait of Hormuz.

Iran's regular army claimed early Wednesday, November 18, 2026, that it targeted Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base with drones. In a statement, Iran's army claimed its drones targeted U.S. forces' Patriot air defense systems as well as drone and missile launch pads at Ali Al Salem Air Base.

This attack is the first to take place in Kuwait since September, according to Reuters.

The General Staff of the Kuwaiti Army announced that the sounds of explosions heard were caused by the interception of the attacks by the country's air defense systems.

Kuwait's Foreign Ministry described the attack as "repeated and criminal" adding that it was a "direct threat" to the Gulf country's "security and stability".

**[ANALYSIS]** Iran has struck Ali Al Salem air base at least 30 times during the war, damaging or destroying at least 15 aircraft hangars, numerous warehouses and 10 buildings near a residential area, according to an Associated Press analysis.

### **CENTCOM CHIEF HOLDS TRILATERAL MEETING IN ISRAEL** [VERIFIED]

US Central Command (CENTCOM) chief Adm. Brad Cooper visited Israel on Tuesday for talks with senior military officials about Iran, as Washington reinforced its regional forces with a third aircraft carrier amid renewed tensions and reports of intensified military operations.

"A trilateral meeting was held between the Chief of the General Staff (Lieutenant General Eyal Zamir), Mossad Director Roman Gofman, and the Commander of Centcom," the military said in a statement.

The visit coincided with the arrival of the USS Theodore Roosevelt in the Middle East, confirmed Monday by a US official. The carrier departed San Diego in September and joins the USS George H.W. Bush and USS George Washington, which arrived in April and August, respectively.

According to a CENTCOM assessment published by *Walla*, the blockade has prevented Iran from exporting more than $25 billion worth of crude oil.

### **MOSSAD CHIEF LOBBIED FOR RESUMED US-ISRAELI STRIKES** [REPORTED]

Roman Gofman, director of Israel's Mossad intelligence agency, advocated resuming joint US-Israeli strikes on Iran during meetings with US officials last week, according to two people familiar with the visit. The visit followed Donald Trump's November 9 pledge to refrain from new strikes after meeting Qatar's prime minister.

Gofman met with officials in US President Donald Trump's administration, including CIA Director John Ratcliffe, in Washington, the sources said. They added that the Mossad director briefed US officials on Israel's intelligence on Iran. Gofman also traveled to Miami to meet Trump's special envoy Steve Witkoff, who has led US diplomacy on Iran and Ukraine.

Around the same time Gofman was in the US, former Israeli minister Ron Dermer, a close Netanyahu confidant, was also in the US advocating for a joint attack in meetings with Trump administration officials.

**White House Response:** A White House official said Trump believed Washington's economic pressure campaign was so "devastating" to Iran that there was no need for "major combat operations."

**[ANALYSIS]** This reveals a significant split: Israel is pushing hard for renewed major strikes while the Trump administration prefers continued economic pressure. The timing — just after Trump's Nov 9 commitment to hold off strikes — suggests Israel is trying to shift US policy before the Democratic Congress takes office in January.

### **SECOND TANKER ATTACKED — ANOTHER SEAFARER MISSING** [VERIFIED]

ADNOC Logistics & Services confirmed that one of its tankers was attacked in the evening on 17 November while transiting the Strait of Hormuz. The company said that one seafarer was missing following the attack and search and rescue operations were ongoing.

According to maritime security firm Vanguard Tech the vessel involved was the Liberian-flagged product tanker Navig8 Messi and it was hit by an unknown projectile around 13 nm north of Khasab, Oman.

It is the second attack this week by Iran on a commercial vessel in the Strait of Hormuz that has resulted in a missing seafarer.

The incident involved the ADNOC tanker brings the total number of confirmed attacks on commercial vessels in and around the Strait of Hormuz to 18 so far in November.

**[ANALYSIS]** Two seafarers now missing in Hormuz attacks this week (Tara Gas and Navig8 Messi). This marks an intensification of human cost in the shipping campaign. 18 vessel attacks in November alone demonstrates Iran is sustaining aggressive Hormuz operations.

### **US TIGHTENS BLOCKADE — TARGETS SHIP-TO-SHIP TRANSFERS** [VERIFIED]

The US military will treat ships that receive cargo at sea from vessels loaded in Iran as attempting to evade its blockade of Iranian ports and could take action against them, according to an advisory issued by US Central Command (CENTCOM) on Wednesday.

"Such ships may be stopped, boarded, diverted, captured, or directed to proceed to the Iranian port where the cargo originated." Ships that fail to comply immediately with lawful orders from blockade or boarding forces could face enforcement action, "including the use of force consistent with applicable law".

The blockade was first imposed on April 13, lifted in June after Washington reached a draft understanding with Tehran, and reimposed on July 14 after the agreement collapsed. Iran has managed to export some liquefied petroleum gas and jet fuel since July, while its crude exports have remained near a standstill for months.

### **KNESSET ELECTS EISENKOT ALLY AS SPEAKER — 66-52 VOTE** [VERIFIED]

Kahana will serve as speaker of the 26th Knesset, defeating Ofir Katz from Netanyahu's Likud Party. Kahana secured the speaker role on Tuesday with the support of the 48 Knesset members of Eisenkot's bloc, which includes the Yashar, B'Yachad, Yisrael Beyteinu and Democrats parties.

Although the Arab-majority parties supported Kahana's appointment as Knesset speaker on Tuesday, Eisenkot's path toward forming a government with their involvement would be a formidable task. As for Netanyahu's bloc, 52 out of the possible 53 Knesset members voted on Tuesday for the Likud's candidate for speaker.

As parliament speaker, Kahana gives the opposition bloc a major legislative advantage, according to Assaf Shapira with the Israel Democracy Institute. "It's important because there is not much time, because the speaker of the Knesset can make the legislative process much, much faster or much slower."

**[ANALYSIS]** This is a major boost for Eisenkot. With speaker control, he has greater leverage over legislative agenda and can fast-track bills if he forms government. The 66-52 margin confirms his bloc + Arab parties = clear majority, but the question remains whether he can translate this into a formal coalition.
