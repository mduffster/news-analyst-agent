## Daily incremental update — US–Israel–Iran conflict  
**Report date:** **Wed, Nov 18, 2026 (ET)**  
**Update window vs prior report:** **since Tue, Nov 17, 2026 (ET)**

### What’s new / what changed (since Nov 17)

1) **Iran expanded direct attacks beyond Bahrain to a U.S.-used air base in Kuwait**
- **What changed:** Multiple outlets report **Iranian drone attacks targeting Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait** (and additional sites in Bahrain). **Kuwait said its air defenses intercepted the drones**; **Iran (state media/IRIB per CBS) said it targeted Patriot air defenses and U.S. drone/missile launch platforms**. **[VERIFIED]** (attack/intercept reported by AP; parallel details via CBS) ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b1f0b530cffe951e6fcc1e27b2e1af94?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** This is a **geographic widening of direct Iran→U.S. basing pressure** (Bahrain → Kuwait), raising the risk of (a) **broader Gulf basing disruption** and (b) **miscalculation** if defenses fail or casualties occur. It also increases the chance partners push for tighter deconfliction—or conversely, for harder U.S. retaliatory action.

2) **Tanker-attack fallout sharpened: ADNOC confirms casualties and a missing crew member**
- **What changed:** The UAE’s state oil company **ADNOC said one crew member is missing and 11 were injured** in a tanker attack while transiting the **Strait of Hormuz** (attack reported as occurring Tuesday). **[OFFICIAL]** (corporate statement reported by AP) ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b1f0b530cffe951e6fcc1e27b2e1af94?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** This is a **material escalation in consequences** (injuries + missing mariner) from the “attack report” phase into **human-cost / potential hostage-rescue dynamics**. It also strengthens the case that Hormuz is shifting from “risk” to **persistent attrition**, which can force changes in routing, insurance, convoying, or rules of engagement.

3) **Shipping behavior and volumes are reportedly changing under threat**
- **What changed:** AP reporting indicates **crude volumes through Hormuz appear to have fallen since early November**, and describes **operational countermeasures** (night transits; minimizing radio/location emissions) by some crews/operators on a U.S.-protected route. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b71b1d25f3cff987b4b125196ecb4c3a?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** If sustained, reduced throughput and risk-premium behavior increases the odds that the conflict locks into a **maritime disruption equilibrium** even absent a major one-off “closure” event.

4) **U.S. posture signal: carrier count in-theater reportedly dropped back to two**
- **What changed:** AP “latest” reporting says the **U.S. military is down to two aircraft carriers in the Middle East**, after a brief period with three (AP notes USS *Theodore Roosevelt* arrived two days earlier). **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/9994c430b2b12b16c03685b981c4830d?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** This could indicate **force-rotation normalization** rather than immediate surge, but against the backdrop of Iran widening attacks, it modestly increases the importance of **land-based air, partner basing, and air defense capacity** for sustained operations.
