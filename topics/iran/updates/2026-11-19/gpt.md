### What’s new / what changed (since Nov 18)

1) **U.S. domestic/political constraint signal strengthened: war-cost scrutiny and base-damage transparency fight**
- **What changed:** AP reports intensifying **Congressional pressure on the Pentagon to release estimates of damage** to U.S. Middle East bases from Iranian missile/drone attacks, amid **war-cost debate** and upcoming **Democratic control of Congress in January 2027**. The same AP piece references **independent estimates** cited in hearings (e.g., blockade cost per day; significant damage claims in Bahrain), with officials declining full assessments due to access limits. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-costs-base-damage-congress-effe3c444d5a5d83a506e47d28add19b))  
- **What it means:** This materially raises the likelihood the administration stays on a **“managed pressure + limited strikes”** track rather than launching a major new strike package—because **budget oversight, hearings, and disclosure demands** will amplify political costs and slow operational freedom (even if tactically the U.S. could escalate).

2) **Force-posture clarity: carrier count and mission described as blockade + limited Hormuz strikes**
- **What changed:** AP details that the U.S. is back to **two carriers** in-theater (USS *George H.W. Bush* and USS *Theodore Roosevelt*) after USS *George Washington* departed; AP also describes carriers as central to a **merchant-shipping blockade** on Iran and notes **limited U.S. strikes** in/around the **Strait of Hormuz**. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-aircraft-carrier-uss-george-washington-f72d5aa085500412989183e606bfadfd))  
- **What it means:** This is less “surge to widen the war” and more “sustain the current campaign design”—maritime/economic strangulation with selective kinetic actions. It reduces near-term odds of a dramatic U.S. escalation driven by force-availability alone, but it also indicates the blockade architecture is **enduring**.

3) **Decision-risk uptick: new reporting that Trump is still considering joint strikes with Israel; chemical/biological angle enters the messaging**
- **What changed:** Axios reports (two sources) that **Trump is still considering** a **joint U.S.-Israel strike** option despite saying recently a new major round was “quite unlikely,” and that Israeli officials are pressing a case tied in part to **chemical weapons concerns**; Axios also reports the White House asked for assessments of Iranian retaliation risks (especially against Gulf energy infrastructure). **[REPORTED]** ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/trump-iran-israel-strikes-chemical-weapons))  
- **What it means:** This adds a **credible “snap-back escalation” pathway**: not because it’s decided, but because planning questions are active and the justification frame may be shifting from “nuclear only” to “CBW risk,” which can be politically potent. Net effect is a modest increase in the probability of a **punctuated escalation event** inside the next 0–60 days—while still not the base case.

4) **Proxy/state-channel management in Iraq: Baghdad–Kataib Hezbollah compromise signals de-escalatory pressure (locally)**
- **What changed:** AP reports Iraq will stand up a **security command HQ in Jurf al-Sakhar** as part of a **compromise** with the Iran-backed **Kataib Hezbollah** militia. **[VERIFIED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/e72472c557057943cab32010666b29bf?utm_source=openai))  
- **What it means:** This looks like an attempt to **reduce an Iraq flashpoint** that could otherwise produce U.S./coalition casualties and widen the conflict. It doesn’t resolve Iran–U.S.–Israel dynamics, but it slightly lowers the chance that **Iraq becomes the next escalation accelerator** in the immediate term.

### Bottom line (what changed in assessment)
- The “**sustained blockade + episodic limited strikes**” pattern looks more entrenched today (carrier posture + war-cost politics), **but** Axios reporting introduces a **non-trivial tail risk** of a renewed major strike decision driven by Israeli lobbying and CBW concerns. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-aircraft-carrier-uss-george-washington-f72d5aa085500412989183e606bfadfd))
