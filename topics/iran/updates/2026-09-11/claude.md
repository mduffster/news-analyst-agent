## Key Developments Since Prior Report (September 10)

### 1. CRITICAL: Houthis Complete Takeover of Bab al-Mandab — Perim Island Seized

**[VERIFIED]** Yemen's Houthis seized on Friday a key Red Sea island in the middle of the Bab al-Mandab strait, completing a push to take control of a waterway crucial to Saudi oil exports, a local government official and eyewitnesses told AFP.

**[VERIFIED]** "Boats carrying armed Houthi fighters reached Mayyun Island after government forces withdrew from it yesterday," the official said, referring to a rocky volcanic island also known as Perim that divides the narrowest part of the strait into two shipping lanes. "The Houthis have completed their takeover of the Bab el-Mandeb area and Mayyun Island, which is located in the middle of the strait."

**[VERIFIED]** The waterway includes four strategic islands: Zuqar, seized by the Houthis on Thursday, Mayyun, and the two Hanish islands. Government military sources told AFP that the Houthis have besieged government troops on the Hanish islands. They said they were unsure about the status of their troops, which number more than 2,000, but believe the Houthis have ordered them to surrender.

**[VERIFIED]** The development is likely to deepen the Middle East war's impact on international commerce, months after the strategic Strait of Hormuz was blockaded by Iran, which hailed its Yemeni allies' advance on Friday.

*Assessment: This is the single most consequential strategic development since the war began. Iran-aligned forces now effectively control both the Strait of Hormuz AND the Bab al-Mandab — the two key chokepoints for global oil transit. This was not an outcome most analysts predicted this rapidly.*

### 2. MAJOR: Trump Rejects Saudi Request for US Strikes on Houthis

**[REPORTED]** Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman called US President Trump twice on Thursday and urged him to launch strikes against the Houthis, Axios reported, citing two US officials. Trump rejected the request, and US officials said the administration does not intend to take direct military action against the Houthis at this time.

**[REPORTED]** Washington will provide Saudi Arabia with intelligence on the Houthis and targeting data, and more than 200 US military personnel are already in Saudi Arabia providing "non-kinetic support." Riyadh said in July that it could deal with the Houthis on its own despite the escalation of the conflict.

**[REPORTED]** Meanwhile, Saudi Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan spoke with his Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi about "the escalation of tensions and the growing insecurity in the region," according to Tehran's foreign ministry.

*Assessment: Trump's refusal to strike Houthis even as they complete the Bab al-Mandab takeover is a major strategic decision. The administration appears focused on limiting the war's geographic scope, but this comes at the cost of ceding a critical chokepoint to Iranian proxies.*

### 3. MAJOR: Saudi East-West Pipeline Reportedly Struck — Unconfirmed

**[UNCONFIRMED]** The East-West Pipeline corridor, also called the Petroline, of Saudi Arabia was targeted by Houthi rebels of Yemen, with multiple satellite images confirming the strikes. However, Saudi Arabian authorities have not issued a statement so far.

**[UNCONFIRMED]** Sentinel-3 satellite imagery shows a black smoke plume over the pipeline route across the desert between Medina and Mahd adh Dhahab. Meanwhile, NASA's FIRMS system also showed clustered thermal anomalies in the area. NASA's FIRMS system gives satellite detections of active fires, but does not identify what is burning or what caused a blaze.

**[REPORTED]** Confidence flag is tagged at maximum, and FRP printing above 70 MW blows way past conventional fire (~30 MW). FRP >70 MW suggests high-pressure crude rupture and blowout conflagration.

*Assessment: If confirmed, this would be catastrophic. The East-West Pipeline is Saudi Arabia's only export route that bypasses the Strait of Hormuz. With Hormuz closed and Bab al-Mandab now Houthi-controlled, damage to this pipeline would effectively block ALL major Saudi export routes. Saudi silence is concerning.*

### 4. MAJOR: US Diesel Prices Hit Record $6/Gallon

**[VERIFIED]** Diesel prices rose above $6 a gallon for the first time ever, raising the risk of further energy-driven inflation just ahead of peak demand season for the fuel. Prices stood at a national average of $6.0556 a gallon, according to the American Automobile Association. In California, they were near $8 a gallon.

**[VERIFIED]** The national average of $6.05 is up from $5.85 last week and $3.70 this time last year, according to motor club AAA.

**[VERIFIED]** Diesel prices are up by 55% since the United States and Israel launched strikes on Iran in February. It is on pace to record the largest annual percentage increase of all time this year.

**[REPORTED]** "Every truck, every delivery, every package, every grocery run just got more expensive," GasBuddy analyst Patrick De Haan said. "Record diesel prices will impact every cargo, shipment, every delivery Americans are taking, and are likely to reignite inflation up and down the supply chain."

*Assessment: This is a direct political cost of the war, with 53 days to midterms. Diesel at $6 will cascade through supply chains, raising food and consumer goods prices. This milestone will be impossible for the administration to ignore.*

### 5. MAJOR: Israel Destroys Hezbollah Ali al-Taher Tunnel Complex

**[VERIFIED]** Using 1,100 tons of explosives, the Israeli military said on Thursday that it had demolished a "strategic" underground facility belonging to the Hezbollah terror group beneath a mountainous ridge in southern Lebanon.

**[VERIFIED]** Army Radio cited Lebanese reports as saying that the country registered a magnitude 4.1 earthquake following the detonation. The underground terrorist infrastructure extended for more than two kilometers.

**[VERIFIED]** Iran last month warned that it would respond forcefully to any attack on Hezbollah's positions on the ridge.

**[OFFICIAL]** Prime Minister Netanyahu and Defense Minister Katz said in a joint statement: "The terror infrastructure and the command center on the ridge were designed to serve as the staging base for the conquest of the Galilee... Their destruction completes the achievement of operational control over the Ali Taher ridge — above and below ground."

*Assessment: Israel proceeded with the tunnel destruction despite explicit Iranian warnings. Tehran has not yet responded — a notable silence that suggests Iran's deterrence credibility has limits when it comes to Hezbollah defensive positions.*

**Net assessment:** The fall of Bab al-Mandab is the most significant strategic development since the war's opening strikes. Both sides continue to avoid American casualties, but the margin for error is narrowing as Iranian missiles grow more accurate.
