- **Houthis captured Mayun (Perim) Island** at the entrance of the Red Sea/Bab el‑Mandeb, confirmed by a Yemeni government military official and a Houthi official. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/025d052a14d9481258d51009a76d0bd6?utm_source=openai))

**What changed / what it means:**  
- **Change:** Yesterday’s key delta was **Mokha**; today adds **Mayun**, which is more directly “chokepoint terrain” than a single coastal city. **[REPORTED]** ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/ab2e69eb2959979bae44eb4d45ae637e?utm_source=openai))
