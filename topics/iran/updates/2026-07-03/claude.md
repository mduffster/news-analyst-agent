### Key Developments Since Yesterday

#### 1. Khamenei Funeral Ceremonies Begin — Scale and Schedule Confirmed

**[VERIFIED]** Coffins containing the bodies of slain Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and several family members are on display in Tehran ahead of funeral processions in multiple locations that millions are expected to attend between July 4-9.

**[OFFICIAL]** Representatives from more than 100 countries are expected to attend the funeral of former Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, which starts on Friday, according to Iranian state-linked news broadcaster IRIB.

**[VERIFIED]** Full schedule confirmed:
- July 3: At a ceremony in Tehran today, foreign delegations are being greeted by senior Iranian officials, including President Masoud Pezeshkian and Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf. July 4: The public part of the funeral begins Saturday 6 a.m. local time when Tehran's Imam Khomeini Mosalla opens for a continuous 24-hour farewell ceremony.
- July 6: This is when the main funeral procession in Tehran will happen. The ceremony is expected to begin at 6 a.m. local time, and the procession, which includes funeral prayers, will follow the 10-kilometer distance from Imam Hossein Square to Azadi Square. July 7: Prayers will be held at the Jamkaran Mosque in Qom.
- July 8: Khamenei's body will be transported to the Iraqi city of Najaf. There will be an official reception attended by Iraq's prime minister, senior government officials and religious leaders before the cortege travels to the Imam Ali Shrine. Afterward, the body will travel by helicopter to the Shrine of Imam Hussein in Karbala, about 80 kilometers away, before flying back to Iran. July 9: Khamenei will be buried at the Imam Reza shrine, one of Shiite Islam's holiest sites, in Mashhad.

**[OFFICIAL]** Airspace over Tehran will be "completely closed" on Monday for the late Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei's funeral procession, Iran's Civil Aviation Organization said on Friday. "On Monday, coinciding with the funeral procession for the late revolutionary leader, Tehran's airspace will be completely closed, and no flights will be permitted."

**[VERIFIED]** The central question of whether Mojtaba Khamenei, the new supreme leader and son of the slain ayatollah, will appear to lead prayers for his father is still unknown.

Mojtaba is believed to have been seriously wounded in that attack, which also killed his mother and his wife. He has remained in hiding since the war began in late February, communicating with his supporters only through written statements, never showing his face or using his voice.

**[VERIFIED]** It remains unclear whether Khamenei's son, Iran's new Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei, will make his first public appearance during the ceremonies. The younger Khamenei, believed to have been wounded in the attack that killed his father, remains in hiding.

**[OFFICIAL]** Iran has warned the US and Israel against attacks during the funeral.

#### 3. NEW: IRGC Commander Vahidi Makes Rare Public Appearance

**[VERIFIED]** Ahmad Vahidi, the influential IRGC commander, made a rare public appearance at Ayatollah Ali Khamenei's funeral after months out of sight. Ahmad Vahidi was seen at meetings overseeing the funeral arrangements before sitting beside Khamenei's casket during a private farewell ceremony in Tehran. A former defence and interior minister, Vahidi has spent decades in the IRGC.

**[REPORTED]** Vahidi is believed to be part of a small inner circle in direct contact with Iran's new Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei, who remains in hiding after reportedly being wounded in the Feb. 28 Israeli strikes that killed his father.

**[VERIFIED]** He has become one of the principal architects of Iran's hardline approach to negotiations over a possible permanent end to the war with the United States. He took charge of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps in the time of war with US-Israel, which killed many of the country's senior military leadership, succeeding Mohammad Pakpour, the IRGC's commander-in-chief, who was killed in the joint US-Israeli strikes on February 28.

#### 4. Hormuz Shipping: Independent Data Confirms Recovery

**[VERIFIED]** Commercial activity in the Strait of Hormuz is recovering despite uncertainty over the crucial waterway's future, according to data from the maritime risk consultancy Marisks. It said 335 vessels transited through the strait last week, while this week is on course for a similar number, with up to 215 transits expected to have been completed by the end of today.

**[VERIFIED]** Convoys are moving under naval escort. 27 ships transited vs ~84/day normal. (Note: This appears to be older data; the Marisks figure of 335/week suggests approximately 48/day — still below normal but improving.)

**[VERIFIED]** Saudi Arabian and United Arab Emirates (UAE) crude shipments have nearly fully recovered to pre-war levels. Crude prices posted a 4.25-month low on Thursday as the recovery in oil flows through the Persian Gulf accelerated, sparking concerns of a supply glut. According to data compiled by Bloomberg, Saudi Arabian crude exports have risen to 6.3 million bpd, or 90% of pre-war levels, in the six days through Wednesday.

#### 5. Oil Rebounds Modestly on Friday

**[VERIFIED]** Brent crude futures rose 0.6% to $72.3 a barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude gained 0.5%, to $69 a barrel.

**[VERIFIED]** Both benchmarks were on track to end the week little changed, with Brent down about 0.02% for the week and WTI up around 0.12%, reflecting a sharp cooling in volatility after the recent geopolitical turmoil.

**[VERIFIED]** Crude Oil fell to 68.56 USD/Bbl on July 3, 2026, down 0.19% from the previous day. Over the past month, Crude Oil's price has fallen 26.31%, but it is still 3.16% higher than a year ago.

**[VERIFIED]** With shipping routes normalising, Gulf producers have begun ramping up exports. Kuwait sharply increased crude production in June to around 1.65 million barrels per day, up from about 580,000 barrels per day in May, following the interim U.S.-Iran peace agreement. Saudi Arabia has also accelerated exports. Shipping data showed at least five supertankers carrying nearly 10 million barrels of Saudi crude have exited the Strait of Hormuz.

#### 6. Lebanon: IDF Operations Continue Despite Framework

**[VERIFIED]** A Hezbollah operative who emerged from the terror group's underground facility at the Ali Taher ridge in southern Lebanon was killed in an airstrike, the Israel Defense Forces said on Thursday. The military said troops of the Egoz Commando Unit spotted a Hezbollah operative emerging from one of the tunnel shafts on the ridge, which is located inside the IDF's south Lebanon security zone. "The terrorist posed an immediate threat to our forces," the IDF said.

**[VERIFIED]** The IDF said it continues to operate at the Ali Taher ridge, beneath which Hezbollah has a massive underground complex, where some 30 operatives are holed up. The military said it will "not allow Hezbollah terrorists to exit the underground tunnel network or move in the area of the ridge."

**[VERIFIED]** A platoon commander in the Golani Brigade's 12th Battalion, IDF Captain David Hazutt, 21, of Ashkelon, was killed on Sunday during operations in southern Lebanon when his unit encountered a Hezbollah operative in the village of Deir Siryan. (This was the first Israeli fatality since the framework agreement was signed.)

**[VERIFIED]** Air raid alerts have dropped sharply in recent weeks. After some 2,092 alerts nationwide on June 7-8 during Iranian and Hezbollah attacks, only 45 alerts were activated between June 9-27, mostly due to hostile UAV infiltrations in northern Israel.

### Emerging Signals

**Mojtaba Khamenei's condition is the critical unknown:** His appearance at any point during the funeral (especially leading prayers) would demonstrate regime stability and his fitness to govern. Continued absence through the burial on July 9 would reinforce concerns that Iran is effectively governed by a military-civilian committee with the Supreme Leader incapacitated.

**Vahidi's emergence signals IRGC dominance:** The boundaries between military command and civilian government are blurring rapidly. The growing assessment is that what is taking shape in Iran is no longer an Islamic Republic in its original sense, but a military junta in every respect. His visible role at the funeral may indicate he's positioned as the de facto decision-maker.

**Oil market has priced in resolution:** With Brent at ~$72 (down from ~$121 peak in April), markets are betting on continued normalization. Any setback in negotiations or renewed Hormuz disruption could trigger sharp repricing.

**Lebanon stalemate stabilizing:** The dramatic drop in air raid alerts suggests both sides have tacitly agreed to minimize escalation even as the framework goes unimplemented. This is neither peace nor war — but it may be sustainable.
